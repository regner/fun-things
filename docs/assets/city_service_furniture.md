# city_service_furniture — Service infrastructure

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine as appropriate. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Dress back streets and utility corners.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_service_furniture.01` — utility cabinet.
- `city_service_furniture.02` — drain cover.
- `city_service_furniture.03` — closed service door and surround.

The door leaf/frame is one static service-entry assembly. Signal Row's current
image does not resolve it clearly; it is a supporting requirement to confirm in
the [district breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/signal-row-assets.md), not evidence
of a working door or a separate new interaction.

## Visual direction and constraints

Quiet metal, broad inset panels, minimal warning colour.

Static closed visual states; no electrical, door, drainage or destructible systems.

Review criterion: **Rear routes feel used without becoming obstacle mazes.**

References: [approved district identities](../concepts/world-v1/stage-03-district-identities/README.md), [District review queue](../concepts/districts-v1/README.md), and the
[shared concept contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md).
District names and copy are provisional. This brief inherits the contract's camera,
flat-terrain, source, state and deferred-dimension requirements.

## Door reuse

Member .03 covers a generic closed rear/service door with surround. It is distinct
from glazed shop entrance leaves and large industrial roller doors. Use it as an
attachment in building openings, and count it once even when a building assembly
includes it. Integral building/module entrance designs remain owned by those kits;
the supporting .03 use does not commission a duplicate for the same opening.
