# d03_court_graphics — Communal court graphics

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Terrace Ward. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Give the open court a memorable surface and small play corner.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d03_court_graphics.01` — large circular paving motif.
- `d03_court_graphics.02` — small play-marking graphic.

## Visual direction and constraints

Both the circular motif .01 and small play-marking graphic .02 are needed
following the district asset pass. Designs remain to refine; no sports equipment
or unique ground-model commission is implied.

Restrained teal/coral/ivory, low contrast across the main walking surface.

Artwork applied to shared paving; not raised obstacles, sports interaction or a new ground mesh system.

Review criterion: **Circular landmark stays visible with benches and people present.**
