# d05_quay_frontages — Narrow quay frontage family

9 October 2026 · **District direction liked; required and supporting uses recorded in the registry.**
Scope: Old Quay. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Create fine-grained irregular waterfront blocks.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d05_quay_frontages.01` — narrow shop-home.
- `d05_quay_frontages.02` — short frontage row.
- `d05_quay_frontages.03` — plum infill house.
- `d05_quay_frontages.04` — deep-roof corner shell.

The [v03 asset breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/old-quay-assets.md) focuses on
`.01–.03`. Give `.01` a narrow shop-home footprint, `.02` a wider/short-row footprint,
and `.03` a compact near-square body with a hipped roof. Plum is a colour option,
not the reason for a unique `.03` model. The deeper corner `.04` remains an option
until a distinct need is selected. Chimney/dormer fittings reuse `city_roof_details`;
do not commission a separate fitting design for each house.

## Visual direction and constraints

Slate roofs, muted plum, amber fronts; varying roof depths.

Mix small plots with civic space. Use shared shop fittings and keep walking passages wide enough for later measurement.

Review criterion: **Quay feels sociable and crooked without uniformly tiny corridors.**
