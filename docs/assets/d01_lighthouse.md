# d01_lighthouse — Northpoint coastal lighthouse

9 October 2026 · **Requested asset; concept design in review. No production commissioned.**
Scope: Northpoint. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Restore the lighthouse from the [original island concept](../concepts/world-v1/stage-01-setting/07-island-ribbon.png)
as a recognisable landmark beside the island boardwalk.

- `d01_lighthouse.01` — complete lighthouse tower assembly, including base doorway,
  tapered shaft, red band, lantern glazing/light appearance, gallery rail and roof cap.

These are parts of one asset, not separate commissions for each visible fitting.
No keeper's house, interior, climbable stair system or rotating-beacon behaviour is
requested. The warm lantern is a material/light appearance within the assembly;
no separate beam-effect asset is needed for this concept.

## Map fit

Use [selected district 01](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#district-01) and
[Northpoint revision 04](../concepts/districts-v1/01-northpoint-v04.png). The proposed
site is in the northwestern coastal strip, landward of the boardwalk near the bend.
Replace a small annex/planting patch locally rather than enlarging the island.
Keep the boardwalk continuous, its seaward edge open, and a clear landward approach.
Existing roads, coastline and district boundary remain unchanged in saved data.

The chosen plot and tower dimensions are illustrative. Check footprint, gallery
overhang and height against the fixed game camera before any later production.
Do not infer a measured site or approved height from the rendered perspective.

## Visual direction and constraints

Slender gently tapered white masonry shaft, broad muted red band near the top,
dark cap and warm glazed lantern. Simple broad forms and restrained joints match
Brackett. Gallery railing belongs to the tower, shaped for its circumference;
the separate shared waterfront rail remains on the boardwalk.

Keep the tower modest relative to the university hall's clock tower, readable from
above without dominating the sports field. No new terrain mound, reclaimed land,
jetty or offshore platform. Use shared paving/grass/planting for the surrounding
approach; those are not unique lighthouse ground models.

Review criterion: **The traditional lighthouse reads clearly beside a continuous
boardwalk while university and sports landmarks remain distinct.**

## Selection and later handoff

The owner explicitly requests this lighthouse. The new rendering remains in review.
Exact bounds, origin, attachment dimensions, material slots and lantern treatment
await refinement. No source/export/prefab, collision, navigation, gameplay camera
acceptance or performance tests exist for it. Modelling and implementation remain
outside the assignment.
