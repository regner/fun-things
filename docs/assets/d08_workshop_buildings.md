## Ironreach revision 02

The owner requests a more rundown district. Use faded petrol paint, localized
rust, worn brick bases, dented shutters and broad mismatched corrugated roof
patches. Retain working amber-lit doors and the existing silhouettes. Reuse wall
bays, roller-door surrounds, roof sections and office interfaces across shed
compositions; weathering variations are finishes, not additional bespoke buildings.
Keep wear readable and stylized rather than adding fine grunge everywhere.
