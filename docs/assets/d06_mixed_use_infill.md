Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d06_mixed_use_infill.02` — stepped corner infill.
- `d06_mixed_use_infill.03` — short rear wing.

## Visual direction and constraints

Quiet slate roofs and warm occupied windows above limited commercial accents.

No Glassward-scale towers. Preserve openings and avoid bridging over foot routes.

Review criterion: **Infill adds rhythm without losing the low hall/plaza focus.**
