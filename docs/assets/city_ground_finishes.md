# city_ground_finishes — Ground and paving finishes

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Supply reusable visual surfaces for courts, yards, gardens and plazas.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_ground_finishes.01` — plain plaza paving.
- `city_ground_finishes.02` — service concrete.
- `city_ground_finishes.03` — parking asphalt.
- `city_ground_finishes.04` — short grass.
- `city_ground_finishes.05` — quiet garden soil.

## Visual direction and constraints

Large low-contrast shapes, no crunchy texture or dense repeating grout.

Visual material/texture family; site shapes are authored layout, not separate duplicate floor models. Keep current flat ground datums.

Review criterion: **Cars and people separate from all five surfaces.**

## Revision 02 asset pass

Ironreach needs .02 for service-yard concrete and .04–.05 for sparse rough planted margins. Broad subtle stains or patches can support the rundown look without dense crack noise. These are visual materials for yards/planting, not road surfaces or a commission for road tooling; parking asphalt .03 remains unselected.

## Surface boundary

Plain paving, service-yard concrete, grass and soil are visual material studies,
not separate floor models per district. District paving briefs own applied motifs
on these shared appearances. Parking asphalt .03 is deferred until the boundary
with future surface tooling is settled; it is not in required demand. Road
carriageway surfaces remain outside this art pass.
