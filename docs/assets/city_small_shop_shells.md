# city_small_shop_shells — Small shop shells

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / Crescents, Terrace Ward, Old Quay, Signal Row, Broadlot. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Avoid commissioning the same ordinary shop once per district.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_small_shop_shells.01` — narrow inline shell.
- `city_small_shop_shells.02` — wide standalone shell.
- `city_small_shop_shells.03` — L-shaped corner shell.
- `city_small_shop_shells.04` — compact square shop shell.

Signal Row v03 uses `.01`, `.02` and `.04` as its three proposed reusable shell
designs. Read `.01` as a narrow-fronted, deep rectangular shop; `.02` can support
one wide tenant or a short multi-tenant frontage; `.04` has a compact near-square
roof and short frontage. Shared entrance/window/fascia fittings provide variation.
The L-shaped `.03` remains a citywide option, not a requirement from this rendering.
See the [image-derived breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/signal-row-assets.md).

## Visual direction and constraints

Ordinary smooth architecture, distinct depth/roof profiles, restrained facade rhythm.

District landmark buildings remain separately owned. Fit frontage attachments from city_shop_fittings; no interiors.

Review criterion: **Variants differ by massing, not colour alone.**

## District shell distinctions

Generic rectangular shops serve Signal Row and Broadlot. The Crescents wedge
landmark d02_corner_shop.01 has a distinct footprint and remains district-owned.
Old Quay's narrow shop-home and frontage row include upper accommodation and
pitched roofs; their assemblies should reuse common wall/window/frontage parts
where compatible without counting generic shop shells as extra buildings there.
The L-shaped .03 is a candidate; Signal Row's older short-row study is an assembly
reference using .02 and shared fittings, not another ordinary wide-shop design.
