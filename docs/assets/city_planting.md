# city_planting — Planters and foliage

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Limited coherent greenery without visual noise.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_planting.01` — low rectangular planter.
- `city_planting.02` — round planter.
- `city_planting.03` — low shrub cluster.
- `city_planting.04` — small broad-canopy tree.
- `city_planting.05` — low sparse weed/rough-grass clump set.

For the [Signal Row breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/signal-row-assets.md),
propose two restrained crown variants of `.04`: compact/upright and broader/rounder.
These share the same city tree language; do not commission a unique tree per placement.
The round planter can also serve as a tree surround, with its planting supplied
separately. Exact bases and foliage density require a closer prop concept.

## Visual direction and constraints

Smooth grouped foliage masses, quiet greens, simple trunks.

No dense leaf texture. Keep trees away from shortcuts and camera-critical corners; no wind system implied.

Review criterion: **Foliage supports scale without hiding the overhead action.**

## Ironreach rough planting

Member .05 is needed along selected fence bases and neglected workshop margins.
Explore short tuft and spreading clump variants with the same quiet olive-green
palette and broad readable blades. Keep them low, sparse and separate from the
existing shrub cluster; vary placement without unique plants for every patch.
Avoid tall grass across gateways, yard centres or walking routes. This is static
visual dressing, not a growth or vegetation simulation. Exact size and camera
readability await an asset concept. Reuse .03–.04 sparingly on public edges;
formal planters are not a required part of Ironreach's rundown direction.
