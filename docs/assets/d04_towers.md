Create a substantial varied skyline.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d04_towers.02` — chamfered-crown tower.
- `d04_towers.03` — rounded-crown tower.
- `d04_towers.04` — mid-height tower.

## Visual direction and constraints

All four tower designs are needed after the district review. Refine shared
facade/floor sections, lobby treatment and compatible crown interfaces so varied
heights and silhouettes reuse parts. Exact module divisions and joins remain
concept work; no per-placement bespoke building or procedural generator is implied.

Indigo masses, cyan crowns, rare magenta corporate accents, broad window groups.

Retain near/above-camera height ambition; exact heights and occlusion acceptance remain pending. Landmark is three unequal members around open ground.

Review criterion: **Crowns differ by shape; the forecourt remains visually important.**
