# city_sign_supports — Civic sign and notice supports

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Reuse hardware while letting districts own their artwork.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_sign_supports.01` — flat wall panel.
- `city_sign_supports.02` — freestanding low panel.
- `city_sign_supports.03` — noticeboard.
- `city_sign_supports.04` — wayfinding post.

## Visual direction and constraints

Simple bevelled frames and generous graphic areas.

Keep copy separate from geometry. Names remain placeholders. No real brands; avoid large panels hiding movement routes.

Review criterion: **Artwork can change without creating a new model for each district.**

References: [approved district identities](../concepts/world-v1/stage-03-district-identities/README.md), [District review queue](../concepts/districts-v1/README.md), and the
[shared concept contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md).
District names and copy are provisional. This brief inherits the contract's camera,
flat-terrain, source, state and deferred-dimension requirements.

## Ownership and reuse

The flat wall panel .01 is a shallow civic/poster panel; freestanding .02 is the
shared low panel body and support used by directories, parking and loading signs.
city_parking_furniture.02 is a use/assembly reference to .02, not another mesh.
Shop fascia frames city_shop_fittings.02 own elongated commercial sign boxes;
choose the appropriate hardware and do not stack two frames for the same face.
Noticeboard .03 serves multi-notice artwork; it is not required wherever a single
wall panel suffices. District graphics own faces/copy and never commission the
same support geometry again.
