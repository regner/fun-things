# d05_civic_graphics — Harbour civic graphics

9 October 2026 · **District direction liked; required and supporting uses recorded in the registry.**
Scope: Old Quay. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Give the waterfront its municipal humour.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d05_civic_graphics.01` — hall fascia art.
- `d05_civic_graphics.02` — noticeboard face.
- `d05_civic_graphics.03` — quay direction panel.
- `d05_civic_graphics.04` — warm shop-front fascia artwork set.

The shop-front set supports the [Old Quay asset breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/old-quay-assets.md):
ordinary fictional waterfront businesses with warm ivory/amber on quiet slate or
plum, broader lettering and less luminous saturation than Signal Row. Reuse the
city fascia frames rather than authoring sign geometry per tenant. Final business
names and artwork count remain open. No new shop interaction is implied.
