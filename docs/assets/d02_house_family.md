# d02_house_family — House and garden-facing home family

9 October 2026 · **District direction liked; required/candidate members recorded in central tracker.**
Scope: The Crescents. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Make winding residential streets varied in footprint and setback.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d02_house_family.01` — detached house.
- `d02_house_family.02` — paired house.
- `d02_house_family.03` — short terrace.
- `d02_house_family.04` — compact corner home.

## Map fit

Use [selected district 02](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#district-02),
not the older island illustration as a placement plan. Gross area 5.26 ha;

inside it. Fit this family alongside the other listed families; this is not its
individual allocation. Exact output quantities await the district arrangement.

## Visual direction and constraints

Blue/slate and muted plum roofs, warm porch accents; distinct roof depths.

The core set is detached, paired and short-terrace designs (.01–.03). The compact
corner home (.04) remains an optional candidate: a rotated existing house does not
require a new model. Include normal domestic doors, window groups, roof planes
and facade trim in these shells; closed appearance only. Reuse the separate
[porch canopy](d02_domestic_details.md) and [chimney/dormer fittings](city_roof_details.md)
where selected. Size/roof variants must distinguish the house types beyond paint.

Do not repeat the same three houses at identical spacing. No detailed interiors; keep cut-throughs visible.

Review criterion: **Silhouettes and setbacks distinguish rows even without colour.**
