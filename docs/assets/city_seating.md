# city_seating — Public seating

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine as appropriate. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Support social corners and waiting areas.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_seating.01` — straight bench.
- `city_seating.02` — short low plaza seat.
- `city_seating.03` — corner seat.

## Visual direction and constraints

Broad continuous surfaces, slate/ivory bodies with restrained trim.

Static dressing; sitting animations and interaction not requested. Leave passage mouths and car exit space clear.

Review criterion: **Visible as low furniture without filling usable open ground.**
