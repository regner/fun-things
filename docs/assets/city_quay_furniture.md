# city_quay_furniture — Waterfront furniture

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / waterfront districts; rail also proposed for the island boardwalk.
Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Common working-waterfront details at different densities.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_quay_furniture.01` — mooring bollard.
- `city_quay_furniture.02` — low quay rail.
- `city_quay_furniture.03` — edge ladder.

## Visual direction and constraints

Simple metal masses; dark teal/slate with minimal working amber.

The low rail `.02` also serves the proposed [island boardwalk](city_boardwalk.md).
Use a consistent dark-metal rail with deck/quay mounting variants and openings at
access points. This reuse does not extend mooring bollards or ladders to every coast.

Decorative ladder does not authorize climbing or swimming. Boundary collision belongs to later integration.

Review criterion: **Quay remains visually continuous and unobstructed.**

## Shared rail kit and terminals

Member .02 owns the shared low rail's straight runs, corner/end returns and posts,
with deck, quay and landward mounting variants to refine together. The landward
rail city_barriers.02 and terminal city_barriers.04 are assembly references to
these parts, not additional unique meshes. Lighthouse gallery and raised-bridge
rails remain within their assemblies because their interfaces and silhouettes
differ; reuse this kit only where a closer concept establishes compatibility.
East Docks' continuous promenade rail is excluded. Selective working-edge safety
rails remain a supporting use to confirm, clear of loading positions.
