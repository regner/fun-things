# city_road_markings — Road and parking markings

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Make the intended street hierarchy readable.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_road_markings.01` — lane line.
- `city_road_markings.02` — stop line.
- `city_road_markings.03` — crossing.
- `city_road_markings.04` — direction arrow.
- `city_road_markings.05` — parking bay.
- `city_road_markings.06` — loading bay.

## Visual direction and constraints

Restrained ivory with amber restricted to working areas.

Artwork only: no new traffic rules. Direction, exact bay dimensions and crossing placement await layout/traffic owner review.

Review criterion: **Markings read at overhead scale without dominating actors.**

These six records are deferred references for future automatic surface tooling,
not active district artwork commissions. District graphics briefs must not create
competing ordinary lane, crossing, parking or loading markings. Keep surface
layout/implementation outside the current concept and asset-brief pass.
