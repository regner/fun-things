# d01_sports_surface — Sports ground artwork

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Northpoint. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Provide the oval track and simple field that identify the campus.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d01_sports_surface.01` — oval track finish and lane graphics.
- `d01_sports_surface.02` — field line graphics.
- `d01_sports_surface.03` — small court graphics.

## Map fit

Use [selected district 01](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#district-01),
not the older island illustration as a placement plan. Gross area 6.40 ha;
287.3 × 347.2 m bounding box, with an irregular boundary and roads/open space

individual allocation. Exact output quantities await the district arrangement.

## Visual direction and constraints

Broad clean markings, field green, restrained warm track tone.

The track finish/lane pattern `.01` and field lines `.02` are needed for the current
Northpoint concept. Reuse [short grass](city_ground_finishes.md), `.04`, beneath
field lines. Small-court graphics `.03` remain a candidate after the main university
hall replaced the earlier small-court arrangement. No sports equipment models are
implied by painted field lines.

Visual surface artwork; no sports mechanics or universal field dimensions assumed. Plot proportions pending owner concept selection.

Review criterion: **Field remains a large quiet shape, not a detailed stadium.**
