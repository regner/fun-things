# city_traffic_fixtures — Traffic fixtures

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine where needed. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Provide reusable physical sign and signal supports.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_traffic_fixtures.01` — signal pole.
- `city_traffic_fixtures.02` — signal head.
- `city_traffic_fixtures.03` — small road-sign support.

## Visual direction and constraints

Chunky readable heads, dark neutral support and simple sign silhouette.

Proposed visual inventory; no signal logic or automatic requirement at every junction. Exact locations and interaction/collision pending integrator.

Review criterion: **Fixtures do not compete with district landmark signs.**
