# city_moored_yachts — Decorative moored yachts

9 October 2026 · **Yachts requested by Regner; silhouettes in concept review.**
Current district demand: Old Quay (05). No other district allocation is implied.
Codex owns the brief/concept; Regner selects the final designs.

## Purpose and deliverables

Populate the [small marina docks](city_marina_docks.md) with a handful of visibly
moored yachts. The image explores six placements from two reusable designs; it
does not require six separately modelled boats.

- `city_moored_yachts.01` — compact motor yacht, hull/deck/cabin assembly.
- `city_moored_yachts.02` — small sailing yacht with simple mast and furled sail.

The motor/sailing mix is a concept proposal in response to the general yacht
request. Share muted hull/deck palette treatments where useful; keep the two
silhouettes different through shape rather than paint alone.

## Map fit and visual direction

Moored inside Old Quay's existing basin, with space between hulls and fingers.
Keep all boats clear of the southern road bridge and open water entrance. No boats
or marina structures outside the harbour are requested by this addition.

Smooth ivory/white hulls, dark petrol glazing, broad quiet deck forms and limited
coral/navy accents. The image prompt explores roughly 10–18 m lengths, not selected
dimensions. Sailing masts/rigging must remain sparse and readable from overhead;
avoid a canopy of fine lines that obscures the docks. Sails stay furled while moored.
Fenders and a minimal mooring-line treatment can belong to each boat assembly;
do not infer a separate nautical-prop catalogue from generated detail.

Review criterion: **Motor and sailing silhouettes read as modest moored yachts,
with the harbour still dominated by open water rather than vessels.**

## Selection and later handoff

Exact X/Y/Z bounds, waterline/freeboard, origin, mast height, berthing gaps and
attachments remain pending concept review. Boats are decorative static scenery
for this phase. No piloting, boarding, sailing, buoyancy, boat interiors, wakes,
damage rules or new vehicle simulation is commissioned.

Source/export/prefab/placement: none created. Rig/animation not requested. Materials,
collision and performance remain unmeasured. Future production/integration owners
are unassigned. Follow the [common brief contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md)
and [handoff template](../templates/asset-handoff.md) if separately commissioned.
