# city_roof_details — Roof service details

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all building districts. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Give roofs believable function with a sparse reusable set.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_roof_details.01` — broad vent.
- `city_roof_details.02` — compact plant enclosure.
- `city_roof_details.03` — roof access housing.
- `city_roof_details.04` — small pitched-roof chimney.
- `city_roof_details.05` — simplified dormer window and roof housing.

The [Old Quay v03 breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/old-quay-assets.md) adds `.04`
and `.05` as candidate reusable fittings for Old Quay, with residential reuse to
consider in The Crescents and Terrace Ward. Keep broad simple forms, roof attachment
and flashing contained in the fitting, and window language consistent with shared
facade windows. Roof pitch, base interface, chimney height and dormer overhang are
pending a close building concept. Neither smoke nor opening/roof-access behaviour
is requested. Do not add them to every roof by default.

## Visual direction and constraints

Large low-contrast forms and subdued dark material.

Decorative only; no rooftop traversal or access. Avoid adding every piece to every roof.

Review criterion: **Building silhouette remains stronger than roof equipment.**
