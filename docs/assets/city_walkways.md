# city_walkways — Sidewalks and walking links

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Connect frontages, crossings and pedestrian escapes.

The proposed [island boardwalk](city_boardwalk.md) owns coastal timber decks and
shore transitions. Connect these walking links to it without duplicating its kit.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_walkways.01` — straight edge.
- `city_walkways.02` — outside corner.
- `city_walkways.03` — inside corner.
- `city_walkways.04` — crossing edge.
- `city_walkways.05` — alley strip.

## Visual direction and constraints

Readable slate midtones, broad seams, restrained bevels.

Existing total corridors 24/17/12/14/7.5 m guide fit. Raised curbs, ramps and collision changes are not decided here.

Review criterion: **Passage mouths remain legible beside buildings and props.**

## Deferred surface ownership

All five records are retained for discovery, with demand deferred until future
surface tooling's scope is decided. They are not a commissioned sidewalk/curb
mesh kit. The current streets provide layout context. Shared paving studies own
appearance; the boardwalk and raised pedestrian bridge own their distinct deck
geometry and connections. Do not commission both walkway floor models and another
surface system for the same routes.
