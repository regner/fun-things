# city_marina_docks — Small marina dock kit

9 October 2026 · **Docks requested by Regner; design in concept review.**
Current district demand: Old Quay (05). Reusable city kit; no other district is
committed to using it. Codex owns the brief/concept; Regner selects the design.

## Purpose and deliverables

Create a few connected berths for moored yachts within the existing harbour basin.
The current [Old Quay concept](../concepts/districts-v1/old-quay.md) explores two
small clusters, not a dense full-basin marina. Cluster count and layout are proposals.

- `city_marina_docks.01` — main floating-pontoon section.
- `city_marina_docks.02` — narrow finger-pontoon section.
- `city_marina_docks.03` — shore-to-pontoon gangway and landing connector.
- `city_marina_docks.04` — dock end/corner connector and edge bumper treatment.
- `city_marina_docks.05` — small dock mooring cleat.

Reuse the shared pedestrian-light design in a restrained dock-compatible treatment;
do not commission another lamp family merely for this placement. Existing quay
mooring bollards belong to [city_quay_furniture](city_quay_furniture.md); small
pontoon cleats have different scale and attachment and are owned here.

## Map fit and visual direction

Use the unchanged Old Quay basin within polygon 05. Explore one west-side and one
northeast-side cluster, each visibly connected to the promenade. Preserve open
central water, the view toward the civic hall and a generous water corridor through
the southern mouth under the existing road bridge. No land reclamation, new harbour
wall or movement of the coastline/road bridge is requested.

Quiet dark timber/composite deck appearance, pale restrained edges, small metal
cleats and sparse warm lamps. Keep broad shapes readable from overhead and reduce
rope/timber detail. Align berths to the [two yacht designs](city_moored_yachts.md),
without using raster boat scale as a measured docking envelope.

Review criterion: **Two modest mooring clusters read as attached to shore while
the harbour's open water and entrance remain visually clear.**

## Selection and later handoff

Exact span lengths, pontoon widths, deck/freeboard height, origin, connector sizes,
water datum, support/mooring treatment and clearances remain pending concept/fit
review. Floating appearance is static scenery here; no tide, buoyancy, docking,
walkable-dock gameplay or water physics is commissioned.

Source/export/prefab/placement: none created. No rig, clips or sockets specified.
Materials, collision, performance and actual-camera acceptance remain unmeasured.
Future production/integration owners are unassigned. Follow the
[common brief contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md) and use the
[handoff template](../templates/asset-handoff.md) if production is separately commissioned.
