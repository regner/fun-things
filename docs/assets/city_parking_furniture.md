# city_parking_furniture — Parking and travel furniture

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / all nine as appropriate. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Support small parking pockets and larger parking sites.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_parking_furniture.01` — wheel stop.
- `city_parking_furniture.02` — parking-sign assembly reference, reusing city_sign_supports.02.
- `city_parking_furniture.03` — cycle stand.
- `city_parking_furniture.04` — simple bus-stop sign.

## Visual direction and constraints

Low quiet forms; broad simple sign faces.

No bus operation or new vehicle commission; signs are optional visual dressing. Parking aisles/clearances remain layout decisions.

Review criterion: **Sparse furnishings preserve open turning and walking areas.**

[shared concept contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md).

flat-terrain, source, state and deferred-dimension requirements.

## Shared sign hardware

Member .02 is a parking-sign assembly reference using city_sign_supports.02.
It does not require another low panel mesh. Broadlot's parking-zone artwork is
owned by d07_retail_graphics.03; generic parking copy elsewhere remains to select.
Member .04 remains a candidate sign arrangement; if selected, first reuse the
small road-sign support city_traffic_fixtures.03 before proposing unique hardware.
Wheel stops and cycle stands remain distinct physical designs.
