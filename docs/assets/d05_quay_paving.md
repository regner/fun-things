## Purpose and deliverables

Tie the hall square and waterfront walking edge together.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d05_quay_paving.02` — simple civic-square inset.

## Visual direction and constraints

Quiet warm-grey paving and restrained teal accents.

Artwork on shared ground; no new seawall, curb or bridge variant. Keep walking openings legible.

Review criterion: **Paving organises the square without covering it in ornament.**
