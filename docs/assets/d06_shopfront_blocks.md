# d06_shopfront_blocks — Signal Row shopping-block family

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Signal Row. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

**Current demand:** broader exploration only. The
[v03 image breakdown](../concepts/districts-v1/signal-row-assets.md) proposes the
shared compact, wide and narrow/deep shop shells for the visible northern buildings.
Do not add this entire family on top of those shared shells without selecting a
distinct row, rear-court or chamfered design in a later concept review.

## Purpose and deliverables

Give the northern social centre short blocks with unequal shop rhythms. The
owner-requested continuous southern building has its own
[shopping-parade brief](d06_southern_shopping_parade.md); do not reuse three detached
shops for that southern strip.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d06_shopfront_blocks.01` — short-row assembly reference, reusing city_small_shop_shells.02.
- `d06_shopfront_blocks.02` — deep rear-court shell.
- `d06_shopfront_blocks.03` — chamfered corner block.

## Map fit

Use [selected district 06](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#district-06),
not the older island illustration as a placement plan. Gross area 2.15 ha;
205 × 199 m bounding box, with an irregular boundary and roads/open space
inside it. Fit this family alongside the other listed families; this is not its
individual allocation. Exact output quantities await the district arrangement.

## Visual direction and constraints

Muted plum walls, quiet indigo roofs, broad fascia areas with selective cyan/magenta.

Ordinary small freestanding shops reuse city_small_shop_shells. Leave visible passage openings; facade variation is not just repainting.

Review criterion: **Roof depths, setbacks and rear access differ across the block.**

## Alternative ownership

This family remains outside the required Signal Row set. Member .01 is an
alternative short-row assembly using city_small_shop_shells.02 and shared shop
fittings; it does not add another ordinary wide-shop model. Rear-court .02 and
chamfered .03 remain optional studies until their distinct massing is selected.
The continuous southern parade is separately required and must stay one building.
