9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**

## Purpose and deliverables

Detached orientation cue across the broad forecourt.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d07_sign_island.01` — low circular island base.
- `d07_sign_island.02` — large restrained sign support.

## Visual direction and constraints

Sparse lime wayfinding, quiet body, simple circle.

Keep low and separate from the retail building. No new traffic island rules or collision defined.

Review criterion: **Reads across open asphalt without becoming a tower.**
