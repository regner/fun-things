# d06_harbour_footbridge — Signal Row / Old Quay pedestrian connection

9 October 2026 · **Owner-requested concept connection; arrangement in review.**
Scope: Signal Row main area, southern shopping strip and Old Quay. Codex owns this
single cross-district brief; Regner selects its visual design. Old Quay references
this record rather than commissioning a duplicate bridge.

## Purpose and deliverables

Provide one connected pedestrian bridge between three destinations: Signal Row's
main northern area, its southern multi-store building area, and the harbour district
to the west. A compact three-arm arrangement is the current concept proposal.

- `d06_harbour_footbridge.01` — shared raised junction deck.
- `d06_harbour_footbridge.02` — main-area connecting span.
- `d06_harbour_footbridge.03` — southern-area connecting span.
- `d06_harbour_footbridge.04` — westward Old Quay connecting span.
- `d06_harbour_footbridge.05` — ground-access stair/landing assembly, adapted at three ends.
- `d06_harbour_footbridge.06` — matching railing, edge-light and support family.

These are members of one assembly, not six separate bridges. Ground access must be
legible at all three ends; the image does not settle exact access construction.

## Map fit

Use the [Signal Row map context](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#signal-row-findings)
and exact polygons 06 and 05. Explore the crossing near the neck between the broad
northern area and narrow southern strip. Landings serve the main plaza-side path,
the northern forecourt of the [shopping parade](d06_southern_shopping_parade.md), and
an Old Quay walking space west of the separating road.

Crossing the district boundary is explicitly requested; moving that boundary is not.
The western end lands in the harbour district on land, not across the harbour water.
This is distinct from the existing [harbour-mouth road bridge](city_harbour_bridge.md).
Keep current streets open beneath the spans. Reserve support and landing footprints
on suitable ground, with no columns in vehicle lanes; any displacement of Old Quay
placeholders must be identified in a later fit study, not hidden by the illustration.

## Visual direction and constraints

Thin pale deck, simple dark teal railings and restrained cyan edge accents. Keep
spans visually light and the junction compact. Stair direction, landing width and
the raised deck should read from the overhead camera. No enclosed roof or large
towers that would obscure the approaches. Keep the existing ground terrain flat.

Review criterion: **A person can visually trace one continuous route to each of the
three ground-level destinations while road continuity remains clear underneath.**

The owner has introduced an elevated pedestrian route into the concept scope.
Deck height, span/clear widths, stair/ramp treatment, vehicle headroom, supports,
occlusion, traversal, navigation and multiplayer implications remain open for later
design/integration. Do not claim the current game supports the bridge or change
gameplay rules during this concept pass. Road-surface generation belongs to future tooling.
