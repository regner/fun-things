# d01_campus_graphics — Campus map and wayfinding graphics

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**

## Purpose and deliverables

Give entrances the district's earnest, eccentric voice.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d01_campus_graphics.01` — campus-map face.
- `d01_campus_graphics.02` — entry panel face.
- `d01_campus_graphics.03` — route-arrow set.

## Map fit

Use [selected district 01](../concepts/districts-v1/map-context.md#district-01),

## Visual direction and constraints

Mint/slate with warm accents; candidate copy: Institute of Almost Knowing.

The entry panel face `.02` is needed; the campus map `.01` and route arrows `.03`
remain supporting uses to confirm in the Northpoint asset pass. No extra clock-face
asset is required: that appearance belongs to the main university hall assembly.

Use city_sign_supports; diagram map and institution name are illustrative pending copy approval.

Review criterion: **Readable graphic hierarchy even when small copy is unreadable.**
