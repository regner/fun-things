## Visual direction and constraints

The owner likes the large arena-looking building beside the field in Northpoint
revision 02. Retain that curved sports-pavilion design and its field-side position
through the university-hall revision. Member `.01` is needed and its broad direction
is liked; `.02` remains a candidate rather than an automatic extra wing. This is
the existing pavilion, not another enclosed arena or new stadium asset.

Broad curved roof with quiet mint entry accents, open field-facing side.

Stay low; protect the adjacent field and walking perimeter. Dimensions await the sports plot fit.

Review criterion: **Landmark remains distinct without a tower.**
