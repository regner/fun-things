# city_harbour_bridge — Harbour entrance bridge

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / Old Quay and city route. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Preserve the single intra-island road crossing over the open harbour entrance.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_harbour_bridge.01` — single level-deck bridge.
- `city_harbour_bridge.02` — matching low parapet pair.
- `city_harbour_bridge.03` — bank-end trim.

## Visual direction and constraints

Simple civic concrete and dark metal with sparse warm fixtures.

Fit the current bridge footprint; no second crossing, new ramp or closure of the water mouth. Structural/collision/under-deck dimensions pending layout owner.

Review criterion: **Bridge reads as a crossing with visibly continuous water underneath.**
