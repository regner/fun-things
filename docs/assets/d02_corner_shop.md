## Purpose and deliverables

Anchor the wedge-garden crescent with a modest local shop.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

## Visual direction and constraints

Blue shop accent, warm entry, roof responding to a curved street.

The wedge shell .01 is needed; the rear annex .02 remains optional and is not
clearly established in revision 02. Proposed site: footprint 29 at the triangular
junction, unchanged in saved data. Use shared glazed door/window, canopy and
fascia fittings. Include the shop’s upper windows, roof and ordinary trim in its shell.

Unique wedge shell; use shared canopy/sign fittings. Gardens are layout using shared ground/planting, not extra bespoke models.

Review criterion: **Curved roof-road-garden relationship is memorable without oversizing the shop.**
