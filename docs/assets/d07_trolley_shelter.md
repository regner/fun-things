# d07_trolley_shelter — Trolley shelter and static trolley group

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Broadlot. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

A recognisable retail detail in a few parking rows.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d07_trolley_shelter.01` — short shelter.
- `d07_trolley_shelter.02` — single trolley.
- `d07_trolley_shelter.03` — compact nested trolley group.

## Visual direction and constraints

All three records are needed for the district set. The nested group .03 is an
arrangement reusing trolley .02, not another unique trolley design. No interaction
or physics is implied; exact group size awaits the detail concept.

Simple dark frame, pale roof, low detail metal basket mass.

Static dressing; no shopping, pushing or loose physics. Preserve parking aisles and pedestrian routes.

Review criterion: **Retail identity survives sparse placement.**
