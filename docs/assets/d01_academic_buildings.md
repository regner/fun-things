## Visual direction and constraints

Slate and field-green roofs; mint wayfinding and amber occupied doors.

The owner requests the traditional university character from the original
[island concept](../concepts/world-v1/stage-01-setting/07-island-ribbon.png).
[Northpoint revision 03](../concepts/districts-v1/01-northpoint-v03.png) explores
one brick-and-pale-stone main hall: formal central arch, ordered tall window bays,
pitched slate roofs and connected wings enclosing an open U-shaped court. A modest
clock tower above the entrance, including its static clock-face appearance, is part of this hall, not a separately commissioned
tower or prop. Likewise its connected wings belong to `.01`; `.02` covers separate
teaching-wing buildings elsewhere in the campus.

Proposed site is the central campus plot west/southwest of the field, replacing
several smaller placeholders around 14/44/42/63/60/51/58/16. The prompt explores
a roughly 45 × 50 m envelope only as visual scale, not measured fit acceptance.
Keep roads, the sports pavilion, track and coastal boardwalk intact. Refine roof,
tower and entrance scale in the fixed game-camera context before later production.
The hall is needed; its revision 03 design remains in review. The teaching-wing and annex members are also needed after the requested district
asset pass; individual designs still need refinement.

Large plots contain smaller buildings and open courts; no equal-block quota or high-rise tower. Use shared roof details sparingly.

Review criterion: **Campus reads from roof/open-space pattern before signs.**
