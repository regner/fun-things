# d08_repair_furniture — Workshop service furniture

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Ironreach. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Give yard edges a practical repair identity.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d08_repair_furniture.01` — tool cabinet.
- `d08_repair_furniture.02` — tyre rack.
- `d08_repair_furniture.03` — low parts trolley.
- `d08_repair_furniture.04` — compact workbench.

## Visual direction and constraints

Broad functional shapes, muted metal, restrained warm accents.

Static closed dressing; no repair interaction, physics clutter or new obstacles across exits. Shared barriers handle boundaries.

Review criterion: **Yard stays visually open with props grouped at edges.**

## Ironreach revision 02

Match the requested rundown character with scuffed painted metal, worn corners
and localized rust. Keep cabinets, racks, trolleys and workbenches usable-looking
and group them by workshop walls or fence edges. Do not add rubble piles or block
yard centres, gateways or the pedestrian bypass. Repeated props share geometry
and use finish variations rather than unique models for every placement.

## Revision 02 asset pass

All four members are needed for the liked revision 02. The tyre rack includes its tyre contents; trolley/workbench storage is restrained integral dressing. Refine one common worn metal palette with distinct silhouettes. No separate loose-tool inventory or moving workshop equipment is required.
