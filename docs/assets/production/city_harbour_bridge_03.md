## Design and dimensions

One reusable bank-end finish: a narrow pale concrete end fascia with a below-datum dark
reveal, plus two short concrete guard terminals with petrol metal coping and broad dark
end plates. It matches the quiet municipal treatment of the completed
[structural deck](city_harbour_bridge_01.md) and [parapet pair](city_harbour_bridge_02.md).
Warm markers remain the parapet's sparse accents; the trim does not add extra beacons.
Old Quay's approved identity and the Stage 4 single level crossing remain the references.
No new ramp, roadway surface kit, pier, second crossing, interior or gameplay system.

The single export is reused at both ends, rather than duplicating symmetric assets.
Local **+X points bankward**, Godot +Y up, +Z across the bridge. Blender +Y maps to
Godot -Z. The source root and joined mesh have identity transforms and metre units.
The attachment pivot is the centre of the bridge end plane at deck height, not the
bottom of the fascia. It is deliberately a **surface/attachment datum**, not a seabed pivot.

Width, shoulder reservations, guard height and deck thickness are inherited from the
siblings. **0.12 m fascia depth, 0.60 m terminal length and detail proportions are
provisional production choices**, not structural/safety certification or an approved
bank layout. These small extensions are bankward; the existing 91 m water span is unchanged.

- East instance: position `(45.5,0,0)`, identity rotation and scale relative to `.01`.
- West instance: position `(-45.5,0,0)`, **180° yaw around Godot Y**, unit scale.
  This is intentional placement orientation, not a corrective import transform.
- Both end planes mate to the siblings' square X=±45.5 joins. The terminal coping and

- Road/sidewalk/marking visuals remain generated by the road tool. The inherited visual
  references Y=0.025 / 0.06 are not new trim surfaces. Reconcile final surface/support
  datums with one continuous collision writer; do not overlap greybox/generated bank
  colliders with this fascia's small top-support strip.
- Trim ends at Y=-0.55, above the deck girders at -1.25 and water reference at -2.1.
  It adds nothing inside the open water span and retains the 0.85 m under-girder gap.
  No navigable-boat or engineered-span clearance is claimed.

## Source, export, materials and prefab

- Source: `art/source/models/environment/city_harbour_bridge_03/city_harbour_bridge_03.blend`.
- Collection `export_city_harbour_bridge_03`; root `CityHarbourBridge03`, joined mesh
  `CityHarbourBridge03_Mesh`. Applied transforms and modifiers; closed manufactured subparts.
- Explicit export: `art/models/environment/city_harbour_bridge_03/city_harbour_bridge_03.glb`
  with pinned-engine `.glb.import` metadata.
- Wrapper: `scenes/prefabs/environment/city_harbour_bridge_03.tscn`.
  `Visuals/Model` is an identity-transform linked imported instance, not embedded mesh data.
- Four opaque back-culling Principled surfaces, in exported order: `bridge_pale_fascia`,
  `bridge_recess`, `bridge_civic_concrete`, `bridge_petrol_steel`. Exact sibling PBR values
  are retained and recorded in validation.json. No textures, embedded images, overrides,
  rigs, sockets, clips, destructive states or real light nodes are required.
- Blender **5.2.2 LTS**, build `d13f752e3b9c`, glTF exporter **5.2.40**. The exporter uses
  `tools/assets/blender/export_settings.json` with named collection/static exclusions.
  Default Godot automatic LOD generation remains enabled; no explicit LOD budget is claimed.

`author.py` reuses `.01/author.py` material/box/finish helpers read-only, preserving the family
manufacturing treatment rather than creating another shared helper library. Rebuilding needs
that sibling's tooling. Render-only context appends the unchanged `.01` and `.02` source
collections; they are never saved or re-exported by this asset's rendering entrypoint.

Collision is separate at `Collision/Body`: **one static body with three boxes**, layer 1 /
mask 0. `Fascia` is size `(0.12,0.55,17)`, centre `(0.06,-0.275,0)`; `NorthTerminal` and
`SouthTerminal` are size `(0.60,1.10,0.35)`, centres `(0.30,0.55,±8.325)`.
The fascia provides continuous support across its 12 cm top; terminals block both side
and nose approaches without detailed bevel/plate snags. No hidden above-road barrier.

Per the mandatory isolated workflow, text-authored prefab and `bank_check.tscn` were loaded,
packed and resaved headlessly. Both roundtrip byte-stably with their generated scene UIDs
and node identities. Godot creates a `.gd.uid` for the check script, not separate `.tscn.uid`
files here. Live owner Blender/Godot sessions were not used or touched; headless import
makes no claim to synchronize a separate open editor scene. The saved fixture composes
existing linked siblings and two trim instances; bank support and actor capsule are
collision-only test geometry, not authored world placement or substitute visual assets.

## Exact reproduction (Git Bash, repository root)

```sh
B='C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe'
T=tools/asset_production/city_harbour_bridge_03
S=art/source/models/environment/city_harbour_bridge_03/city_harbour_bridge_03.blend
O=C:/tmp/ft/assets/city_harbour_bridge_03
export ALSOFT_DRIVERS=null SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy
# Rebuild/export only when not editing the source.
timeout 300 "$B" -noaudio --background --factory-startup --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 --python "$T/author.py"
timeout 180 "$B" -noaudio --background --factory-startup --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 "$S" --python "$T/export.py" -- "$O/reexport"
timeout 180 "$B" -noaudio --background --factory-startup --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 --python "$T/validate.py" -- "$O/reexport/city_harbour_bridge_03.glb"
timeout 900 "$B" -noaudio --background --factory-startup --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 --python "$T/render.py"
timeout 300 "$(mise which godot)" --headless --editor --path . --import --quit
timeout 180 "$(mise which godot)" --headless --editor --path . --script "res://$T/check.gd" -- --normalize
timeout 180 "$(mise which godot)" --headless --path . --script "res://$T/check.gd"
timeout 30 "$(mise which gdstyle)" "$T/check.gd"
# Resolve binaries before Windows mise shims; UTF-8 avoids cp1252 decoding failures.
GODOT_DIR=$(dirname "$(cygpath -u "$(mise which godot)")")
STYLE_DIR=$(dirname "$(cygpath -u "$(mise which gdstyle)")")
# The checker requires a fresh external output directory on every run.
PATH="$GODOT_DIR:$STYLE_DIR:$PATH" PYTHONUTF8=1 timeout 1800 python tools/production_checks.py --output "$O/checks-repro"
python "$T/manifest.py" "$O/checks-repro"
```
