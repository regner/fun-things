# d03_apartment_family — Stepped apartment and infill family

9 October 2026 · **District direction liked; modular construction required. No production commissioned.**
Scope: Terrace Ward. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Build open communal courts from varied mid-rise groups assembled from one shared
apartment kit. The owner explicitly requests modular parts rather than one-off
buildings. The district overview establishes character; it does not prove that its
rendered buildings already share workable modules.

Keep the existing four IDs as composition references. They describe arrangements
of the shared kit, not four bespoke whole-building mesh commissions:

- `d03_apartment_family.01` — L-shaped apartment assembly reference.
- `d03_apartment_family.02` — U-shaped open-court assembly reference.
- `d03_apartment_family.03` — short infill assembly reference.
- `d03_apartment_family.04` — stepped-bar assembly reference.

Proposed reusable module designs, all covered by this brief:

- `d03_apartment_family.05` — straight apartment bay, one storey, with standard wall/window treatment.
- `d03_apartment_family.06` — corner bay set, with compatible inside/outside corner treatment.
- `d03_apartment_family.07` — finished end bay / exposed wall closure.
- `d03_apartment_family.08` — ground-floor entrance bay with stair-core facade treatment.
- `d03_apartment_family.09` — balcony / loggia frontage insert with railing.
- `d03_apartment_family.10` — roof-cap set with straight, end and corner variants.
- `d03_apartment_family.11` — height-step closure for exposed upper-storey walls and roof junctions.

These seven module records are a concept proposal; set entries may contain several
compatible shapes. They are not a claim that seven meshes or fixed dimensions
have already been validated. Normal window frames and facade trim belong to their
bay modules; no separate unique door/window commission for each complete building.

## Modular assembly approach

Choose a common bay width, building depth, storey height, origin and connection
datum during module concept refinement, using the selected plots and game camera.
Do not set a city-wide grid or distort the map to fit the kit. No numeric grid is
accepted from this image. Repetition must preserve window proportions; adjust bay
count and setbacks rather than stretching individual windows.

| Composition | Shared parts and variation |
| --- | --- |
| L group | Two runs of straight bays joined by corner treatment, finished ends and entrance bays |
| Open U court | Three connected runs using the same bays/corners; keep the court mouth and side walking link open |
| Short infill | Fewer straight bays, two ends, entrance and compatible roof caps |
| Stepped bar | Vary stacked storey counts along a run; use the height-step closures and matching roof caps |

Use repeated bays, different run lengths, stepped heights, entrances and selective
balcony inserts for variation. Blue-grey/teal/coral material choices share the
same geometry. Roof caps should cover the selected low-pitched/flat silhouettes
without a unique full roof for each assembled building. Ground entrances replace
compatible base bays; their core treatment is exterior only, not an interior or
stair gameplay requirement.

The next asset concept review should show the parts together with at least two
different compositions made from those same parts, including a height change.
Check corner/end closures, window alignment, roof joins and open court access in
those concept drawings before separately commissioning production. Do not treat
the current district raster as evidence of seamless joins or measured fit.
