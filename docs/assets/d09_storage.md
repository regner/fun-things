# d09_storage — Dock storage masses

9 October 2026 · **District direction liked; required and supporting uses recorded in the registry.**
Scope: East Docks. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Supply sparse, organised freight dressing.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `d09_storage.01` — long container.
- `d09_storage.02` — short container.
- `d09_storage.03` — low covered stack.
- `d09_storage.04` — simple crate group.

The first concept groups low containers and crates at warehouse and apron edges. Avoid stacking across the open centre between cranes or obscuring the service spur and ordinary street footways. Exact stack arrangements remain preliminary.

## Contents and repeated arrangements

All four members are needed. Long/short container assemblies share corner-post,
end-door and corrugated-panel language; dimensions still need refinement. Covered
stack .03 includes its low stacked contents and restrained cover. Crate-group .04
includes one reusable crate design with small arrangement variations, not a unique
crate for every placement. Pallet/support appearance belongs to these low storage
assemblies unless a later prop concept establishes another reusable requirement.
