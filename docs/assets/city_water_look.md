# city_water_look — Water appearance brief

9 October 2026 · **Draft concept brief; not selected or commissioned for production.**
Scope: Shared / coastal districts. Brief/concept author: Codex; selection: Regner.

## Purpose and deliverables

Unify the sea and harbour behind the city.

Each member below is covered by this brief; these are proposed unique outputs, not
placed quantities or committed runtime IDs. Repeated placements reuse a member.

- `city_water_look.01` — open sea surface look.
- `city_water_look.02` — quieter basin surface look.

## Visual direction and constraints

Cool dark blue/teal with broad restrained reflections, no busy foam field.

Visual study only. Shader, mesh, tide, physics and containment work are outside this phase.

Review criterion: **Water supports warm/cool district accents without competing with streets.**

References: [approved district identities](../concepts/world-v1/stage-03-district-identities/README.md), [District review queue](../concepts/districts-v1/README.md), and the
[shared concept contract](../concepts/districts-v1/brief-contract.md).
District names and copy are provisional. This brief inherits the contract's camera,
flat-terrain, source, state and deferred-dimension requirements.

## Required visual studies

Open-sea appearance .01 is one shared study used by coastal districts 01, 02, 03,
04, 05, 07, 08 and 09. Signal Row is inland. The calmer basin variation .02 is needed
only by Old Quay (05); it is not a separate water system per district. Exact
reflection treatment remains to refine with the city's fixed lighting. These
records own visual direction, not water geometry, shader or simulation work.
