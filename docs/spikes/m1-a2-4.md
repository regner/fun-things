# M1-A2.4 — player lifecycle, safe respawn, and match reset

11 October 2026. This row adds one authoritative player-lifecycle owner to the saved Match and
uses it for standalone, host, and replica presentation paths.

## Delivered behavior

- `PlayerLifecycle` owns alive/dead state, the authoritative admitted roster, an exact 180-tick
  respawn delay at 60 Hz, a bounded ten-candidate safe-spawn search, and a 300-tick search failure
  deadline. `SpawnReservations` makes overlapping planar spawn claims atomic within the tick.
- MatchReplication combines those reservations with a physics overlap query, neutralizes dead
  actors before publishing death, disables their collision and presentation, and advances the
  existing entity generation when the retained participant respawns.
- Match reset increments the match revision, closes stale command/state paths, rebuilds admission
  under the new revision, clears old spawn claims and command history, and immediately rehydrates
  retained participants at safe authored anchors. Session participant identities remain unchanged.
- Reliable lifecycle hydration drives replica alive/dead presentation, respawn countdowns and the
  HUD roster. Input and movement envelopes retain session, match-revision and entity-generation
  fences; replicas never become lifecycle writers.
- Standalone now enters through MatchReplication instead of Boot spawning a separate direct-authority
  player, so first playable and network authority share lifecycle and reset rules.

## Verification coverage

GUT unit coverage exercises reservation conflict/release, exact respawn timing, alternate and failed
safe-spawn searches, retained-player reset hydration, entity-generation advancement, saved Match/HUD
wiring, admission timeout continuity, and RPC channel registration. The separate-process ENet harness
also drives death, respawn and retained-peer reset and requires both processes to report the new
match revision and generation transitions. After reset it waits for another 0.5 metres of replicated
authority movement, proving the generation-scoped command window and client input gate reopened.
