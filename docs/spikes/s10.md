# S10 — host-owned pedestrian AI foundation spike

8 October 2026. This is a bounded technical spike over the accepted partial S06
intersection, not production M1-C3 population AI. It uses capsule presentations and
measures deterministic host simulation only. Animation, networking, the six-block
city, final traffic priority, final body dimensions and subjective behavior remain
open.

## Question and constraints

Can 64 host-owned pedestrians wander an explicit sidewalk/crossing graph, react to
localized gunfire and an all-agent flee storm, coexist with a moving crossing car,
and retain at most 16 dead presentations while leaving a credible share of the
provisional 4 ms host-simulation p95 budget?

The fixture keeps S06 as the topology owner. The harness admits the current undirected
west–crossing–east foot route, its three semantic link identities, each partial
sidewalk/crossing route and S06's derived road map through public APIs. Population owns
AI state, death retention and replenishment; `S10Pedestrian` only applies a requested
displacement and presents a capsule. The harness never lets AI write health or car
state, adds no RPC and does not change S06. A saved fixture instances the unchanged
West/East sectors and derived bake, and saves Population, DeadPresentations and a
crossing-car assembly. Runtime instantiates the saved pedestrian scene, rather than
constructing authored actors in code.

## Research

- Godot's [NavigationAgent3D documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_navigationagent3d.html)
  separates path following from avoidance, requires callers to move the parent, and
  warns that avoidance has a significant performance cost with many registered agents.
- The [Using NavigationAgents guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/navigation_using_navigationagents.html)
  says not to request a new path every frame and describes `velocity_computed` as the
  safe-velocity handoff when avoidance is enabled.
- Godot's [navigation performance guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/navigation/navigation_optimizing_performance.html)
  recommends reducing unnecessary path updates, agent counts and avoidance work; map
  synchronization and queries are separate costs.
- [CharacterBody3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_characterbody3d.html)
  owns scripted collision motion through `move_and_slide`/`move_and_collide`; this is
  convenient but does broadphase/query work that direct graph motion can avoid.
- Van den Berg et al.'s [ORCA/RVO2 work](https://gamma-web.iacs.umd.edu/ORCA/) gives a
  principled collision-free reciprocal-velocity method. Its assumptions are not a
  gameplay crossing-priority policy, and dense crowds can still look overly coordinated.
- Fiedler's [Fix Your Timestep](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/)
  motivates a fixed simulation step and bounded per-step work rather than varying AI
  behavior with render rate.

These sources support fixed-rate, bounded updates and separating path semantics from
local motion. They do not select Petrol & Coral's crossing priority, panic tuning or
acceptable crowd look.

## Options

| Option | Fit and trade-off | Spike disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S06 explicit foot graph + constrained kinematics + simple spatial separation | Crossing identities and legal corridor remain inspectable. No navmesh synchronization/path request is needed for this flat route. Direct transforms are cheap but do not prove obstacle sweeps, slopes or final collision. | Implemented as `graph_kinematic`; proposed semantic foundation, with production shape queries still required. |
| Same graph/AI + `CharacterBody3D.move_and_collide` for every pedestrian every tick | Reuses an engine collision boundary and is easiest for local solid contact. It still needs graph crossing semantics and host AI; 64 bodies pay query/motion cost even on an unobstructed route. | Implemented as `character_body` for the cost comparison. |
| NavigationServer3D navmesh + NavigationAgent3D/RVO avoidance | Better free-area detours and dynamic avoidance tools. A navmesh alone does not encode marked-crossing permission, car priority, dead retention or replenishment, so semantic graph/state remains necessary. RVO cost and behavior need a representative free-area fixture. | Researched, not built as a second planner. Revisit only when six-block content requires free-area detours. |

**Decision proposal:** retain an explicit semantic foot graph. At 64 pedestrians,
run 60 Hz constrained motion, stagger ordinary perception/route decisions at 10 Hz,
and use a bounded spatial grid. Production should shape-cast only when a link has a
nearby solid/car/wreck or when steering leaves its free corridor; it must not copy the
prototype's direct-transform path as collision-complete. Keep NavigationAgent/RVO out
of the initial flat-route implementation unless real six-block free-area detours show
that graph links plus local steering are insufficient.

For the starting crossing policy, pedestrians wait before entering while a car is
within the conflict gap; a pedestrian already exposed flees and dies on actual car-box
contact. Cars do not yield in this fixture. This is a technical proposal, not owner
ratification. A car-yields alternative improves pedestrian throughput but makes traffic
AI reserve and brake for crossings; a wait-for-gap policy keeps one reservation owner
and avoids pedestrians stopping an uncontrolled player car, at the cost of queues.

## Prototype and reproducibility

`tests/fixtures/s10/benchmark.tscn` is the saved S06 composition. `pedestrian.tscn`
is a 0.38 m radius, 1.8 m high capsule-only CharacterBody. `population.gd` owns:

- 64 live slots whose spawn poses, graph progress, lookahead tangent, endpoint reversal,
  lateral projection and replenish edges derive from the admitted 251-point S06 route;
- three independently queried legal regions (`approach_west`, `crossing`,
  `approach_east`) and four derived S06 road strips used to check real capsule poses;
- bounded local spatial separation and 10 Hz staggered perception while every live slot
  moves at 60 Hz;
- 20 s localized gunfire, 120 s blast/death events, five-second panic and a separate
  all-population threat every five seconds;
- a saved 1.8 x 3.4 m car crossing at 8 m/s, gap waits and lethal box contact;
- five-second edge replenishment and a FIFO cap of 16 dead capsule presentations;
- per-event stable target identities captured from real publication-time positions;
  every expected reaction is required within six perception phases, and a runtime
  negative verifies that zero of 64 reactions fails closed;
- per-tick AI+movement wall time and sampled legality, stuck, overlap and reaction data.

Each option/scenario/seed simulates exactly 36,000 ticks (ten minutes). Lethal car
contact stops during the final five-second replenish-settlement window, while AI and
car movement continue. The options
share all AI and metrics; only direct constrained displacement versus
`move_and_collide` changes. Pair overlap is sampled once per simulated second outside
the timed section. “Stuck” means the identity's endpoint displacement is below 0.25 m
across a four-second window; it can therefore flag oscillation as well as immobility.

The editor was unavailable. The two minimal format-3 scenes were hand-authored as the
allowed fallback, imported with the pinned engine, loaded/resaved headlessly, assigned
engine-generated resource UIDs and inspected. No existing scene or imported asset was
modified.

From the repository root, the one end-to-end measurement command is:

```sh
export PATH="$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which godot)"):$PATH"
rm -rf C:/tmp/ft/lanes/s10-pedestrian-ai/reproduction
timeout 3000 python tools/s10/run.py \
  --godot "$(command -v godot)" \
  --output C:/tmp/ft/lanes/s10-pedestrian-ai/reproduction
```

The runner requires a fresh external output, verifies exact
`4.8.dev7.official.c971f93e7`, stages only S06/S10 and S06 model dependencies into an
addon-free project, imports it, applies a 900 s deadline to each owned Godot child and
writes `result.json`. It terminates/kills only a timed-out child it launched. The
committed accepted receipt is [s10-evidence/result.json](s10-evidence/result.json).

## Results

All twelve corrected runs completed: seeds 101/202/303 x normal/flee storm x both
motion options. Every run retained current S06 content and the exact foot links
`approach_west`, `crossing`, `approach_east` (251 full-route points, three compiled
legal segments and four road segments). All ended with 64 live slots, kept at most 16
dead presentations, matched every per-event expected reaction within the six-phase
bound, and recorded zero off-sidewalk or in-road-outside-crossing agent-ticks from
actual body positions. No engine/script warning or error appears in the accepted
isolated logs.

Timing is a **contended upper bound**, not quiet-machine evidence. The three seed
snapshots saw 5/3/3 Godot processes before and 6/4/4 during sampling. Other lanes were
active. Instantaneous `Win32_Processor` snapshots ranged 2–83% and are retained
but are not continuous utilization. A quiet rerun is required before a capacity gate.
Values below are median across the three per-run summaries, with the worst repetition
shown separately; they are not falsely pooled percentiles.

## Interpretation: proven and not proven

**Proven in this bounded fixture**

- Current S06 public queries drive all traversal progress, endpoint reversal, lateral
  projection, semantic crossing admission and replenishment poses for 64 host AI slots.
  The admitted route's three link identities and continuous legal regions fail closed.
- Independent graph-derived sidewalk/crossing segments and S06 road strips classify
  actual body positions; all corrected runs have zero illegal agent-ticks.
- Every publication-time target identity reacts exactly once within 83.33 ms. Storm
  runs prove all 64 targets x 120 events = 7,680 reactions, while the explicit
  zero-reaction negative fails with both missing-count and absent-reaction diagnostics.
- Death/replenishment work remains finite and dead presentations never exceed 16.
- With identical AI, corrected graph p95 is lower in all six paired measurements and
  both aggregate medians. Contention prevents attributing the whole difference to the
  motion call, and the direct option does not thereby become collision-correct.
- Simple separation plus a crossing gap wait is inadequate at this density: both
  options produce large overlap and stuck counts, and normal runs suffer many car hits.

**Not proven**

- The proposed <=1.0 ms pedestrian p95 production share. Every accepted contended run
  misses it; even normal graph p95 is 2.654 ms median across repetitions.
- Quiet Windows timing, Linux timing, real four-player/traffic/chain integrated cost,
  six-block paths, navmesh/RVO cost, final collision sweeps, slopes or dynamic wrecks.
- Good-looking or collision-free stampedes. Pair samples are once/second; they are not
  continuous overlap integration. Capsule visuals are not the shared animated S13 rig.
- Continuous global count 64 after deaths. This one-corridor fixture drops to 38–47
  during its five-second safe replenish delay and then returns to 64; production needs
  distributed out-of-view slots and safe candidate admission.
- Product approval of pedestrian-priority versus car-priority crossings, panic values,
  death rate, replenishment delay or behavior feel.

## Proposed budget and M1 production requirements

Reserve **<=1.0 ms p95 and <=2.0 ms p99** of the host's provisional 4/8 ms
simulation budget for all 64 pedestrian AI, route, local avoidance and movement work
in normal play. During a one-tick global threat fanout, permit the same sustained
share by queueing/staggering perception; do not create a separate unbounded storm
budget. These are proposed subsystem allocations, not a relaxation of the whole-host
budget. The current prototype misses both targets under contention and therefore is
not production-ready.

M1-C3 should:

1. Keep Population as sole slot/AI/replenishment/dead-retention owner; use Match-owned
   identities/lifecycle and existing motion/health/collision owners.
2. Keep explicit foot links and crossing IDs from CityData. Add a finite crossing
   reservation/gap API rather than inferring legality from road polygons in each agent.
3. Use structure-of-arrays or compact typed state and a reusable spatial grid; this
   dictionary-heavy measurement fixture is diagnostic, not the implementation model.
4. Run ordinary perception/route decisions at a staggered 10 Hz while applying shared
   motion at 60 Hz. Threat publication carries host tick, position, radius, kind and
   bounded lifetime; target selection must have a per-tick cap.
5. Replace direct transform movement with bounded swept capsule/clearance queries near
   obstacles, cars, actors and wrecks. Measure a selective-query implementation against
   always-CharacterBody motion before selecting it.
6. Implement a finite conflict queue/reservation at crossings and flow-aware separation.
   Add tests that cap wait time, overlap duration and queue length; this spike's naive
   wait/separation result is explicitly unacceptable.
7. Define car contact through authoritative relative motion/sweep. Dodge only when a
   legal corridor exists; otherwise stop/flee/die once, fence movement on terminal life
   revision and never let presentation decide damage.
8. Maintain 64 global live slots through distributed, out-of-view safe spawn candidates
   where possible, with bounded retry and an observable deficit when no safe candidate
   exists. Never spawn merely to make the counter green.
9. Retain at most 16 dead pedestrian presentations independently of gameplay tombstones;
   retirement must not permit stale movement or damage to resurrect an identity.
10. Reduce far perception/path refresh based on relevance, not authority: all pedestrians
    continue authoritative 60 Hz collision/motion needed for cars and threats. Far LOD
    may stagger decisions/animation but cannot pause global outcomes.
11. Re-run three-seed ten-minute normal/storm measurements on a quiet machine, then in
    the full 64-pedestrian/32-car/four-player/chains representative load. Record actual
    query counts, crossing queue age, continuous overlap duration and path work.
12. Replicate committed pose/life only through Replication; clients never run population
    outcomes. Late join installs life/collision before movement and no historical panic
    presentation is replayed as a new event.

## Open owner questions

- Should AI traffic yield to marked-crossing reservations, should pedestrians always
  wait for a gap, or does priority vary by crossing? The fixture uses wait-for-gap only.
- Is a temporary observable live-population deficit acceptable when every safe spawn is
  blocked, or must another already-live out-of-view pedestrian be reassigned first?
- What crowd look is desired during blasts: loose individual fleeing, lanes with bounded
  overtaking, or a denser stampede? The technical result rejects the current overlap,
  but choosing the visual/behavior target remains a product decision.
