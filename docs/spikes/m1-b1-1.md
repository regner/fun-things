# M1-B1.1 vehicle motion, replication and prediction

10 October 2026. This production slice ports the accepted S04 planar handling rule into
`VehicleMotion` without loading or modifying the archived fixture. The port keeps one step for
standalone, authority, replay and future AI callers, typed `DriveCommand` admission, and the
owner-ratified decision-30 `VehicleTuning` defaults. It also carries decision 29's complete planar
handbrake deceleration and planar-speed-only handbrake steering authority.

The delivered Latch, Crate and Sable wrappers remain source-linked below each vehicle entity's
`PresentationAnchor`. Each entity has a distinct, model-sized `BoxShape3D` envelope and authoritative
root sockets copied from the delivered wrapper values. `VehiclePresentation` consumes only motion
telemetry to steer the imported front wheel pivots and spin all four imported wheel pivots.

The follow-up production slice uses that same `VehicleMotion.step()` for host authority and client
replay. Match creates the three parked-car EntityRefs from authored anchors, sends reliable bounded
descriptors before movement, admits fixed 16-byte drive packets under assignment-derived control
epochs, and publishes consumed-or-superseded acknowledgements. Local control restores authority and
replays at most 120 retained inputs while visual correction remains on `PresentationAnchor`; remote
cars use the shared extrapolate, hold and blend policy. Control loss explicitly unbinds prediction
and makes the body passive. The temporary host-side driver assignment seam exists only for these
real-process tests; M1-B1.2 replaces it with seat transactions.

`scenes/dev/vehicle_drive.tscn` is the replacement standalone tuning entry. It authors all three
vehicle instances, the Brackett Match world, a displaced source-linked flat ground sector, the 47 m /
42 degree camera and HUD. Keys 1-3 select a car; F11 switches flat/Brackett starts; F1-F10 and +/-
adjust tuning; F12 persists `user://vehicle_tuning.tres`; Backspace restores decision-30 defaults.
Project-owned drive actions map throttle to W/Up, reverse to S/Down, steer to A/D or Left/Right,
brake to Shift and handbrake to Space. The harness enforces a 60 FPS cap.

No Godot editor was available for the original standalone worktree. The new saved scenes were
authored directly as text, then loaded, instantiated, packed and resaved with pinned Godot 4.8-dev7
before their resource UIDs were registered and reimported. Prediction, network binding and remote
presentation are now included. Seats, entry/exit, doors and durable vehicle lifecycle transactions
remain with later B1 rows.

Validation receipts are indexed in
[`m1-b1-1-evidence/README.md`](m1-b1-1-evidence/README.md). The replication acceptance run passed
205/205 tests with 8,800 assertions; the post-review epoch-preflight run passes 207/207 with 8,815.
Separate-process direct/normal/adverse ENet runs prove authoritative and local
straight, turn, brake, reverse and handbrake outcomes plus wall and moving-car contact. Raw correction
p95 is 0.018526 m direct, 0.305130 m normal and 0.822337 m adverse against the unchanged 0.5 m target.
The adverse miss is retained rather than hidden or retuned away; see the evidence index for its
transition/replay analysis and candidate tuning levers.
