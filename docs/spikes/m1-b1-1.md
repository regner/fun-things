# M1-B1.1 standalone vehicle motion port

10 October 2026. This production slice ports the accepted S04 planar handling rule into
`VehicleMotion` without loading or modifying the archived fixture. The port keeps one step for
standalone, authority, replay and future AI callers, typed `DriveCommand` admission, and the
owner-ratified decision-30 `VehicleTuning` defaults. It also carries decision 29's complete planar
handbrake deceleration and planar-speed-only handbrake steering authority.

The delivered Latch, Crate and Sable wrappers remain source-linked below each vehicle entity's
`PresentationAnchor`. Each entity has a distinct, model-sized `BoxShape3D` envelope and authoritative
root sockets copied from the delivered wrapper values. `VehiclePresentation` consumes only motion
telemetry to steer the imported front wheel pivots and spin all four imported wheel pivots.

`scenes/dev/vehicle_drive.tscn` is the replacement standalone tuning entry. It authors all three
vehicle instances, the Brackett Match world, a displaced source-linked flat ground sector, the 47 m /
42 degree camera and HUD. Keys 1-3 select a car; F11 switches flat/Brackett starts; F1-F10 and +/-
adjust tuning; F12 persists `user://vehicle_tuning.tres`; Backspace restores decision-30 defaults.
Project-owned drive actions map throttle to W/Up, reverse to S/Down, steer to A/D or Left/Right,
brake to Shift and handbrake to Space. The harness enforces a 60 FPS cap.

No Godot editor was available for this worktree. The new saved scenes were authored directly as text,
then loaded, instantiated, packed and resaved with pinned Godot 4.8-dev7 before their resource UIDs
were registered and reimported. This slice deliberately excludes prediction history/reconciliation,
network bindings, seats, entry/exit, doors and lifecycle transactions; those remain with later B1/A2
rows.

Validation receipts are indexed in
[`m1-b1-1-evidence/README.md`](m1-b1-1-evidence/README.md). The production tests independently cover
command rejection, shared-stream equivalence, wall/reverse/service-brake behavior, decision-29
handbrake behavior, the relative slide threshold, per-model composition and wheel presentation.
