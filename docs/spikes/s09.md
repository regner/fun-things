# S09 — host-owned traffic AI foundation

8 October 2026. This is a bounded research/prototype result, not production M1 traffic,
physics capacity, damage, population lifecycle or a ratification of car feel. The accepted
base at lane start was `4fa669aa947b41ca4f8827b43c93db82322eb338`. The pinned engine was
`4.8.dev7.official.c971f93e7` and gdstyle was 0.3.0.

## Question and constraints

Can a host-owned controller follow directed lanes, serialize crossings and recover from a
stopped player car or wreck while sending only `(throttle, steer, brake, handbrake)` to the
unchanged S04 handling owner, within a useful share of the 4 ms p95 / 8 ms p99 host
simulation envelope? The target population is 24 moving traffic cars plus eight parked cars
within 32 live cars. Cars abandoned by players must remain parked; population replenishment
must be bounded and cannot visibly pop near any player.

This spike measures 24 and 32 **moving** controllers as a conservative AI-work comparison.
It does not change the M1 composition of 24 moving plus eight parked. It uses a saved small
test graph rather than claiming the neutral S06 intersection is a production district.

## Research

- Godot's [AStar3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/classes/class_astar3d.html)
  supplies weighted point/edge search, while
  [NavigationAgent3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/classes/class_navigationagent3d.html)
  follows NavigationServer paths and optional local avoidance. Neither representation alone
  owns directed lane legality, turn identity, junction priority or S06's stale-bake contract.
- Coulter's [Implementation of the Pure Pursuit Path Tracking Algorithm](
  https://www.ri.cmu.edu/publications/implementation-of-the-pure-pursuit-path-tracking-algorithm/)
  describes geometric lookahead tracking. The Stanley controller's heading/cross-track
  approach is documented by Thrun et al. in
  [Stanley: The Robot that Won the DARPA Grand Challenge](
  https://doi.org/10.1002/rob.20147). Pure pursuit has fewer terms for this low-speed arcade
  case; Stanley offers stronger explicit cross-track correction but needs speed/low-speed
  treatment and more tuning.
- Treiber, Hennecke and Helbing's
  [Intelligent Driver Model](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.62.1805) combines desired
  speed, time headway and relative-speed braking. The maintained
  [IDM explanation](https://traffic-simulation.de/info/info_IDM.html) makes its assumptions
  and parameters inspectable. A simple bounded time-gap rule is smaller for this spike but
  does not reproduce realistic queue waves or heterogeneous drivers.
- SUMO documents explicit
  [intersection right-of-way](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/Intersections.html) and
  [dynamic rerouting](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Demand/Automatic_Routing.html). These support
  keeping route legality, junction ownership and rerouting as separate decisions rather
  than treating local obstacle avoidance as permission to leave the road.
- Valve's Left 4 Dead
  [AI systems presentation](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf)
  describes population placement constrained by player visibility and travel flow. For a
  listen server, replenishment must test all admitted player cameras plus authoritative
  clearance/threat state; one client's render notifier cannot own spawn safety.

The cited robotics/traffic sources are models, not claims that production traffic needs
realistic autonomous-driving behavior. M1 needs readable arcade traffic and bounded host
work.

## Options and decision proposal

### Lane representation and path following

| Option | Fit and trade-off |
| --- | --- |
| NavigationServer3D/NavigationAgent3D | Useful for unconstrained free-space agents and avoidance. It does not by itself encode directed lanes, legal turns, stable lane IDs, S06 content revision or junction ownership. Dynamic avoidance can also produce off-road vehicle motion. Do not select as the car route owner. |
| Custom directed graph + pure pursuit | Reuses S06's explicit directed topology and emits steering from a lookahead point. Small, inspectable and compatible with shared S04 drive commands. It can cut corners and needs speed-aware lookahead/lane-error checks. **Recommended for M1 traffic.** |
| Custom directed graph + Stanley | Explicit cross-track plus heading control can tighten lanes. More tuning and low-speed handling than this top-down fixture warrants. Keep as a measured fallback if final car dimensions/handling cannot satisfy lane bounds with pure pursuit. |

Production should query S06/CityData's directed graph and revision, use speed-aware pure
pursuit to create a command, and let the same S04 handling/body owner used by players apply
that command. AI must never set a car transform, velocity or alternate physics constants.

### Following, intersections and recovery

| Concern | Compared choices and proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Car following | Full IDM gives smooth relative-speed behavior and realistic queues; a fixed-distance rule is too abrupt. Start with the prototype's bounded speed-sensitive time gap, then add relative closing speed if actual body tests show oscillation. Do not add IDM parameters without a measured need. |
| Intersection | Traffic lights are predictable but unnecessary for M1. Gap acceptance permits concurrency but is harder to prove safe. A bounded reservation queue ordered by accepted host tick then EntityRef is deterministic and lifecycle-fenceable. **Use reservation/priority initially**, allowing compatible movements together only after conflict sets are authored and tested. |
| Blockage | Teleporting or local off-road avoidance violates lane ownership. **Wait, brake, short bounded reverse, then reroute through authored links**; retry/replan limits follow the S06 contract. After repeated failure, park/report the car. Cleanup/replenishment is a Population/lifecycle action and only occurs outside every player's protected visibility region. |
| Replenishment | Never recycle an active visible car just to meet a target. Select an authored traffic spawn outside every admitted camera frustum plus a conservative margin, validate lane direction and current physics clearance, and use a bounded candidate/rate queue. If no candidate is safe, remain below cap and retry later. |

## Prototype

`tests/fixtures/s09/network.tscn` is a minimal saved graph with a central four-way
intersection, two closed crossing loops, a nonconflicting outer loop, a scripted stopped
player car and a wreck. Base routes have saved alternate controls around each blockage.
The editor was unavailable, so the allowed direct-file fallback hand-authored this minimal
format-3 scene; the pinned engine assigned and persisted its scene UID and both script
dependency UIDs during a headless load/resave, and headless import generated script sidecars.
No project setting, prior fixture or S04/S06 source was changed.

`S09TrafficSimulation`:

1. admits the saved route IDs and authored conflict centers;
2. distributes a bounded seeded population (six cars exercise the two crossing/blocked
   loops; remaining cars exercise sustained following and turns on the outer loop);
3. uses pure-pursuit steering, a speed-sensitive same-lane gap and one reservation owner per
   conflict zone;
4. waits two seconds, reverses for 0.5 seconds, binds the authored detour, and reports
   recovery only after clearing the obstruction envelope and sustaining forward progress;
5. calls unchanged `S04DriveRules.advance` once for every car on every 60 Hz tick; and
6. records full per-tick AI wall time, provisional S04 oriented-footprint overlap episodes,
   intersection-local no-progress/gridlock episodes, recovery and nearest centerline error.

The prototype integrates the velocity and yaw returned by `S04DriveRules`; it does not
instantiate `S04Kinematic`, call `move_and_slide`, run engine collision or apply damage.
Thus it proves one handling-rule owner and controller workload, not actual CharacterBody
contacts or final handling. Overlap uses planar separating-axis tests for the provisional
S04 1.8 by 3.4 metre envelope after a safe bounding-circle broad phase. Independent parallel
and perpendicular regressions exceed the retired 1.7 metre center threshold while still
being detected. This is envelope evidence, not engine contact evidence.

`tools/s09/run.py` stages an addon-free project containing S09 and only the unchanged S04
rule owner. It imports once, then launches six isolated cases: populations 24/32, seeds
11/29/47, exactly 36,000 ticks (10 simulated minutes) each. Each case retains all timing
samples and records Windows CPU load and concurrent Godot process count before launch.
`tools/test_s09.py` independently checks nearest-rank aggregation uses full tick samples.

One end-to-end reproduction command, from repository root with a fresh external output:

```sh
export PATH="$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which godot)"):$PATH"
timeout 900 python tools/s09/run.py --godot "$(command -v godot)" \
  --output C:/tmp/ft/lanes/s09-traffic-ai/reproduction
```

## Results

Accepted evidence is indexed in [s09-evidence](s09-evidence/README.md). All six processes
passed public checks and exited cleanly. The runner retained unchanged copied-source
fingerprints. No case reported engine/script warnings or errors.

| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24 | 3 / 108,000 | 0.820 / 1.749 / 2.826 / 158.204 ms | 0 | 0 (0) | 6 total | 18/18, all 3.6 s | p95 1.478/1.478/1.478 m; worst 1.560/1.560/1.559 m |

The per-car no-progress events are temporary reservation/following waits. The separate local
tracker watches only crossing-route cars within each conflict/queue region, so outer-loop
motion cannot reset it. It records started, resolved and unresolved four-second episodes and
maximum episode duration; the measured cases had none. A retained regression holds local
cars still while outer traffic moves, observes one unresolved episode, then resolves it only
after local displacement. All six blocked-loop cars per seed cleared the provisional obstacle
plus car half-diagonal margin, moved at least one metre on the detour and then sustained at
least 1 m/s forward progress for 0.5 seconds. Detours stay active, so each car contributes one
successful 3.6-second recovery rather than repeatedly meeting the permanent obstruction.

Every timing row is a **contended upper bound**. The 24-car launches observed 4/6/6 total
Godot processes including the measurement and instantaneous 71/68/54% CPU load; 32-car
launches observed 5/3/5 processes and 14/3/45% CPU load. The low CPU snapshots do not make
those launches quiet: concurrent Godot count and retained long stalls show contention. One
32-car tick reached 323.639 ms and is retained rather than suppressed. Aggregate 32-car p95
is 2.115 ms and individual seed p95 is 2.252/2.091/2.029 ms. A quiet rerun is required before
treating this workstation result as hardware capacity.

### Proposed budget share

Reserve **traffic AI/controller p95 <= 2.0 ms and p99 <= 4.0 ms at the M1 24-moving-car
cap** as a provisional implementation ceiling, inside—not in addition to—the host's 4/8 ms
complete simulation budget. The contended 24-car aggregate and each seed meet it, but this
is a production target and quiet remeasurement entry criterion, not a passed M1 budget. Use
**p95 <= 3.0 ms / p99 <= 5.0 ms** only as a 32-moving-car stress guard;
that row is deliberately above M1's moving population and narrowly meets the guard under
contention. At the actual 24 moving plus eight parked mix, parked cars must perform no
controller/path work; their lifecycle/query costs still count in the integrated host budget.
If quiet/integrated evidence exceeds the share, first replace all-pairs gap and collision
scans with lane/conflict-zone occupancy indexing; do not reduce population or simulation
frequency silently.

## Proven, not proven and production requirements

Proven within this deterministic fixture:

- null and malformed non-null topology configuration fail atomically and leave `run()`
  unavailable rather than publishing partial route state;
- every simulated car used the unchanged shared S04 rule exactly once per tick;
- saved directed loops, turns, bounded same-lane following and two reservation zones ran for
  three ten-minute seeds at both populations;
- all 36 scripted player/wreck encounters cleared the obstruction envelope and resumed
  sustained detour progress in 3.6 seconds;
- provisional S04 oriented-footprint overlap and intersection-local gridlock counts were
  zero, with independent positive overlap and masked-local-gridlock regressions; and
- full-sample timing, contention and source identity are reproducible and retained.

Not proven: actual `CharacterBody3D` contacts/slide response, S06 production graph binding,
final car dimensions/handling, damage/wreck lifecycle, AI-to-player seat transfer, parked
or abandoned behavior, replenishment/visibility, network replication, reset/late join,
64 pedestrians, six-block routes, drawn traffic, animation/audio, quiet hardware, integrated
4/8 ms host budget, memory/bandwidth, Windows/Linux exports or Deck performance.

M1 production must:

1. make Population the sole host owner of the global 24 traffic + eight parked slots, spawn/
   cleanup queues and replenishment rate; occupied and abandoned cars remain within 32;
2. bind stable EntityRefs and current S06 content/life/control/collision revisions before an
   AI controller can command a car;
3. query one directed CityData lane/turn graph and authored conflict sets; never runtime-bake,
   invent an off-road bypass or create an AI-only transform/velocity writer;
4. emit only validated finite S04 drive commands and run the same body/handling path used by
   player intent, including prediction-compatible command semantics without AI replay effects;
5. bound route searches, lane occupancy, reservation queues, accepted work per tick, retries
   and cancellation; order contested grants by accepted tick then EntityRef;
6. test relative speed/gap, actual swept body clearance and compatible intersection movements
   through production public APIs, including opposing, queued and damaged cars;
7. implement finite wait/reverse/replan/park reporting, cancel it on seat transfer, destruction,
   disconnect, reset and retirement, and fence every delayed callback by current revisions;
8. atomically stop AI before granting a player the car; after exit/death/disconnect the surviving
   car coasts to a stop and remains parked—AI never resumes automatically;
9. use authoritative clearance/threat checks and all admitted camera frusta plus a reviewed
   margin for spawn/cleanup; bounded failure leaves population below target rather than popping
   visibly or exceeding caps; and
10. rerun actual-body route/contact/recovery, quiet traffic timing and integrated host/network/
    lifecycle capacity on named target hardware before M1 acceptance.

## Open owner questions

- Ratify simple reservation priority for M1, or require lights/authored stop priority and
  compatible concurrent movements for the six-block district.
- Choose the protected visibility margin and retention time for replenishment/cleanup after
  final camera speed/FOV and district occlusion behavior are accepted.
- Decide whether temporary reverse motion needs a visible warning/horn; this spike selects no
  presentation or audio policy.
- Final car dimensions/handling and S06 route clearances remain separate owner decisions; an
  affected actual-body rerun is mandatory after either changes.
