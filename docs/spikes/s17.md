# S17 — integrated host-tick composition

9 October 2026. This is a bounded integrated foundation measurement, not production M1
capacity acceptance. It composes unchanged S04/S05/S09/S10/S11/S12 spike owners in one
headless saved host scene at the simultaneous M1 population caps. The accepted lane base
was `4abc07d73500cba9abec714b108c194fda5bfe1e`; the pinned engine was
`4.8.dev7.official.c971f93e7`.

## Question and verdict

At 64 pedestrians, 24 moving traffic cars, eight parked cars, four firing players, 16
rockets, 16 wrecks and 16 dead pedestrians, does the composed host work fit the provisional
60 Hz simulation budget of p95 <= 4 ms and p99 <= 8 ms, and where is time spent?

**Contended verdict: over-subscribed at p95, within p99.** Across three seeds and 108,000
complete ticks, total median/p95/p99/worst was **2.999/5.233/6.901/37.544 ms**. The p95
misses 4 ms by 1.233 ms (30.8%); p99 retains 1.099 ms of margin. Every repetition observed
other Godot processes, so these are **contended upper bounds**, not a quiet hardware gate.
The result justifies production optimization and a quiet rerun; it does not prove that the
same composition fails on an otherwise idle target machine.

The main p95 costs were pedestrians (**2.455 ms**) and three complete S11 encodes
(**2.069 ms**). Traffic was **1.286 ms**, inside both the audit's approximate 1.5 ms share
and S09's later 2.0 ms proposal. Pedestrians missed S10's proposed 1.0 ms share. Snapshot
encoding deliberately repeats all 148 rows for each of three clients every tick as the S17
brief requires; this is more CPU and hypothetical traffic than S11's selected-rate schedule.

## Research and measurement choices

- Godot's [`Time.get_ticks_usec`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/classes/class_time.html#class-time-method-get-ticks-usec)
  is monotonic and is the engine API used around each subsystem. Its integer microsecond
  granularity is sufficient here, but each short section includes timer-call overhead.
- Godot's [CPU optimization guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/performance/cpu_optimization.html)
  recommends profiling before optimization and distinguishes script, physics and rendering
  costs. This fixture records explicit script-owner sections; it is not a replacement for
  production profiler captures.
- Godot's [idle and physics processing documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/scripting/idle_and_physics_processing.html)
  defines fixed-rate physics callbacks. The prototype advances deterministic 60 Hz rules in
  the same order each tick but accelerates wall time, so it measures work rather than pacing.
- Glenn Fiedler's [Fix Your Timestep](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/)
  explains why fixed simulation steps need enough headroom to avoid falling behind. A median
  pass cannot compensate for sustained p95 over-subscription.
- Glenn Fiedler's [Snapshot Interpolation](https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_interpolation/)
  motivates rate selection and buffering rather than sending every complete state every
  simulation tick. S11's measured schedule remains the relevant bandwidth design; S17 uses
  deliberately conservative repeated encoding to expose CPU composition.

## Options considered

| Option | Fit and trade-off | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add isolated S09/S10/S11/S12 percentile summaries | Cheapest and leaves every fixture untouched, but percentiles are not additive and do not capture allocator/cache contention or coordinator overhead. | Rejected as an integrated verdict. Isolated rows remain diagnostic comparisons. |
| One accelerated saved host scene with timer brackets | Runs deterministic full-cap owners sequentially for three ten-minute seeds, retains every tick sample, and attributes cost. It omits real pacing/network service and Jolt stepping between accelerated ticks. | **Selected for this foundation spike.** |
| Real-time production-like listen server with three clients and profiler capture | Highest fidelity for physics, transport, prediction and scheduler interaction. It needs production owners and would take at least 30 wall-clock minutes for three seeds before setup/fault profiles. | Required later for M1-D3, not invented inside S17. |

Production should keep one host coordinator with explicit command-before-simulation and
capture-after-simulation phases, instrument owner sections in development builds, and repeat
this budget on real production bodies/networking. Do not treat a sum of isolated subsystem
p95 values as a capacity result.

## Prototype

[`host.tscn`](../../tests/fixtures/s17/host.tscn) is the single saved composition. It
instances the unchanged S09 lane topology, S10/S06 pedestrian benchmark, S12 targets and
12 S05/S04 car bodies, and saves the S03 session, S12 combat owner and S05 damage owner.
The editor was unavailable. The minimal format-3 scene was text-authored under the brief's
fallback, imported with the pinned engine, and assigned its retained scene/script UIDs. No
S03-S12 file was edited.

[`S17Host`](../../tests/fixtures/s17/host.gd) measures, with `Time.get_ticks_usec`, this
order on each of 36,000 ticks per seed:

1. S10 normal-play event, graph route, local separation, graph-kinematic movement and
   death/replenishment work for 64 slots;
2. S09 reservation, following, blockage recovery and unchanged `S04DriveRules.advance`
   for 24 moving cars (eight parked cars do no controller work);
3. S12 target history plus one scripted validated/rewound shot per player every 15 ticks;
4. S05 chain work, with one 12-car chain restarted every 7,200 ticks (five per seed); and
5. one complete 148-row S11 encode repeated independently for each of three clients.

The inclusive `total` bracket starts before pedestrians and ends after snapshot encoding.
Row construction is included in the snapshot section. The fixed population rows also include
four players, 16 synthetic moving rockets, 16 retained wrecks and 16 retained dead
pedestrians. Rockets/retained rows exercise codec cardinality only; they have no projectile,
collision or presentation simulation.

### S17-local adapter seams

S09 exposes only monolithic `run()` and S10 only monolithic `run_case()`. S12's direct host
validator is behind its RPC boundary and named `_validate_intent`. Per the approved S17
integration direction, the read-only adapters call the existing S09 `_reset_case`, `_spawn`
and `_step`, S10 `_reset`, `_spawn_initial_population` and `_tick`, and S12
`_validate_intent`, `_rewound_position` and `_ray_hits`. They do not copy those algorithms.
Only S10's graph-input assembly was copied line-for-line from
`tests/fixtures/s10/benchmark.gd` at base `4abc07d`, with attribution in source.

Each run first performs a 120-tick equivalence probe. S09's adapter matched public `run()`
with zero pose error and 2,880 drive-rule calls. Two separately instanced S10 saved fixtures
had zero pose error and equal metrics. S12 returned `OK`, `STALE_SEQUENCE` and `INVALID`
for valid, duplicate and nonfinite cases. These checks support fixture equivalence; the
private methods are **not accepted production APIs**. Accelerated S12 work explicitly
advances its wall-clock cadence/reload gates according to simulated cadence, rather than
waiting in the timed loop.

The runner stages only required fixtures/assets into an addon-free project, strips the main
scene/autoload/editor-plugin settings, imports once, and starts one owned headless process
per seed. It records concurrent Godot process count and CPU-load snapshots before/during/
after each process and stops only an owned child on timeout. Complete tick samples remain in
three per-seed JSON files; pooled nearest-rank aggregation is independently unit-tested.

Canonical reproduction command (fresh external destination required):

```sh
export PATH="$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which godot)"):$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which gdstyle)"):$PATH"
timeout 3600 python tools/s17/run.py --godot "$(command -v godot)" \
  --output C:/tmp/ft/lanes/s17-host-tick/reproduction
```

## Results

All three processes exited zero with no engine/script diagnostics. Every equivalence and
outcome check passed. Each repetition ended with 64 pedestrian slots/live agents, at most 16
dead presentations, zero graph-legality violations, 864,000 S04 drive-rule calls, zero S09
footprint collisions/gridlocks, and 9,600 accepted S12 shots/rewind queries. Each of 15 total
chains completed 12 explosions and 144 target visits with target-work peak four and queue
peak five. Each seed encoded 216,000 packets, maximum 1,196 bytes.

Complete pooled samples (108,000 ticks):

| Section | Median ms | p95 ms | p99 ms | Worst ms | Proposed share / result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Pedestrians | 1.234 | **2.455** | 3.432 | 30.577 | 1.0 ms p95 / **miss** |
| Traffic | 0.590 | **1.286** | 1.860 | 27.920 | audit ~1.5; S09 2.0 / pass both |
| Combat/history | 0.042 | 0.116 | 0.208 | 9.621 | no prior integrated share |
| Explosions/chains | 0.006 | 0.012 | 0.028 | 4.157 | no prior integrated share |
| Three snapshot encodes | 1.016 | **2.069** | 2.860 | 34.948 | no prior integrated share |
| **Inclusive total** | **2.999** | **5.233** | **6.901** | **37.544** | 4/8 ms / **p95 miss, p99 pass** |

Per-seed total p95 was 5.220/5.090/5.354 ms and p99 was 7.015/6.757/6.917 ms. This is a
repeatable p95 over-subscription under the observed contention, not one bad seed.

Contention observations were 3/2/2 Godot processes before and 4/3/3 during the three runs,
including the measurement process. Instantaneous Windows CPU load was 46/1/41% before,
77/5/31% during and 5/60/16% after. These snapshots do not establish continuous utilization;
every timing row is labelled **contended upper bound**. A quiet rerun remains mandatory.

Evidence is catalogued in [s17-evidence](s17-evidence/README.md). `result.json` holds the
pooled result and telemetry; the three seed files retain all timing samples. Logs and SHA-256
bindings are committed. The earlier isolated import failure caused by a staged `run/main_scene`
reference is not part of accepted evidence; the runner now strips that setting and the
accepted import is diagnostic-free.

## Interpretation and budget proposal

This composition exposes over-subscription earlier than M1-D3, but it does not justify
lowering population, tick rate or the 4/8 ms whole-host target. The cheapest production
changes are architectural rather than balance changes:

1. **Replication:** build one immutable row set and encode one shared payload when all three
   clients receive the same selection; fan out the same packet bytes. Retain S11's bounded
   rate buckets/revisit bound instead of encoding every entity every 60 Hz tick. Add
   per-client work only after relevance differs. Target **<=0.75 ms p95** for row capture +
   all outgoing encodes at three clients.
2. **Pedestrians:** replace dictionary-heavy slot state with compact typed/structure-of-arrays
   data, retain staggered 10 Hz decisions, and use a reusable spatial grid/selective queries.
   Preserve 60 Hz authoritative movement/collision. Target S10's **<=1.0 ms p95**.
3. **Traffic:** retain the measured directed-graph/S04 path and target **<=1.5 ms p95** at
   24 moving cars. Use indexed lane/conflict occupancy before reducing fidelity if the real
   body implementation exceeds it.
4. Reserve **<=0.25 ms p95** for four-player combat/history, **<=0.10 ms p95** for chain/
   lifecycle work, and the remaining **0.40 ms** of the 4 ms envelope for body physics,
   prediction replay, session work and coordinator overhead. This last reserve is narrow;
   production profiling may require reducing the first three shares rather than relaxing the
   whole-host budget.

These shares sum to 4 ms and are ceilings inside the whole-host budget, not independently
spendable allowances. p99 must remain <=8 ms and production should also track worst/hitch
causes without redefining p99 around them.

## Proven and not proven

**Proven within this fixture**

- one saved composition executes full declared population cardinality for three deterministic
  ten-minute seeds and retains every section/total tick sample;
- S09/S10/S12 private-seam adapters reproduce short source-fixture outcomes before timing;
- 24 traffic controllers call unchanged S04 rules once/tick while eight parked rows do no AI;
- five S05 12-car chains per seed finish through the unchanged damage/chain owner;
- four scripted players traverse S12's actual shape/admission/sequence/cadence/ammo validator
  and target-history query path without a network transport;
- three complete S11 encodes per tick remain <=1,200 bytes per packet; and
- under observed contention, p95 exceeds 4 ms while p99 remains below 8 ms, with pedestrian
  and repeated snapshot encoding as the largest attributed p95 sections.

**Not proven**

- quiet Windows or any Linux timing, named target-hardware acceptance, real-time pacing, or a
  graphical/exported process;
- engine physics/Jolt stepping per accelerated tick, 32 moving `CharacterBody3D` contacts,
  selective pedestrian sweeps, player movement/prediction/replay or actual rocket motion;
- socket/ENet service, packet send, transport allocations, bandwidth or adverse delivery;
- production S11 rate selection/shared-payload caching, real state schema or lifecycle journal;
- four-client joins, reset, seat transitions, damage from S12 into S05, AI/player contacts,
  rendering, animation, audio or VFX; or
- a production public per-tick integration contract. Calling underscore-prefixed fixture units
  is an explicitly bounded measurement adapter, not architecture to ship.

The fixture both overestimates production snapshot CPU (three full all-entity encodes every
host tick) and underestimates total host work (no real body physics, prediction, sockets or
projectiles). Those effects cannot be assumed to cancel.

## M1 production requirements

1. Add public per-tick APIs for traffic, pedestrians and combat validation/history; preserve
   one owner per field and remove every production dependency on S17's private fixture seams.
2. Define and enforce one host phase order: accepted commands, AI decisions, shared movement/
   physics, damage/lifecycle, then state capture/replication. Use explicit process priorities
   when engine callbacks participate.
3. Retain per-owner and inclusive tick instrumentation in development builds with bounded
   sample export and nearest-rank median/p95/p99/worst aggregation.
4. Implement shared immutable S11 row capture/packet reuse plus measured rate buckets; include
   reliable lifecycle separately and never trade correctness for the encode budget silently.
5. Implement compact pedestrian state/indexed occupancy and traffic lane/conflict indexing,
   then measure actual selective queries and CharacterBody contacts at full caps.
6. Integrate local foot/car prediction replay, four player command streams, S12 damage handoff,
   rockets, S05 chains, seats, retained entities and reset without duplicate side effects.
7. Run at least three real-time ten-minute seeds on named quiet Windows and Linux M1 hardware,
   then repeat normal/adverse three-client ENet profiles. Record network service, Jolt, AI,
   replay, combat, chains, replication and total separately.
8. Keep whole-host p95 <=4 ms and p99 <=8 ms. If a share misses, optimize ownership/data/query
   work first; changing caps, tick rate or budgets requires owner review.
