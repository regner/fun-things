# S17 — integrated host-tick composition

9 October 2026. This is a bounded integrated foundation measurement, not production M1
capacity acceptance. It composes unchanged S04/S05/S09/S10/S11/S12 spike owners in one
headless saved host scene at the simultaneous M1 population caps. The accepted lane base
was `4abc07d73500cba9abec714b108c194fda5bfe1e`; the pinned engine was
`4.8.dev7.official.c971f93e7`.

## Question and verdict

At 64 pedestrians, 24 moving traffic cars, eight parked cars, four firing players, 16
rockets, 16 wrecks and 16 dead pedestrians, does the composed host work fit the provisional
60 Hz simulation budget of p95 <= 4 ms and p99 <= 8 ms, and where is time spent?

**Contended verdict: over-subscribed.** The ratified run used a 3,600-tick unmeasured warmup
and then retained 36,000 measured ticks for each of three seeds. Across 108,000 measured
ticks, the deliberately conservative case—three complete 148-row encodes every tick—had
total median/p95/p99/worst of **3.527/6.519/8.643/186.217 ms**. It misses both the 4 ms
p95 and 8 ms p99 limits.

As a clearly labelled comparison, substituting S11's production schedule—one broadcast
74-row subset every four ticks—produced total median/p95/p99/worst of
**2.394/4.582/6.286/105.321 ms**. The production-schedule line still misses p95 by 0.582 ms
but passes p99 by 1.714 ms. It reuses each tick's measured common-owner duration and swaps
the separately measured snapshot duration, avoiding a second execution of stateful owners.
It is a CPU schedule comparison, not a transport or production acceptance result.

Every repetition observed other Godot processes before launch or during startup/warmup, so
both lines are **contended upper bounds**, not quiet hardware gates. No contention command
ran during a measured window. The result justifies optimization and a quiet rerun; it does
not prove failure on an otherwise idle target machine.

The main conservative p95 costs were pedestrians (**2.960 ms**) and three complete S11
encodes (**2.478 ms**). Traffic was **1.423 ms**, inside the audit's approximate 1.5 ms
share and S09's 2.0 ms proposal. Pedestrians missed S10's proposed 1.0 ms share. The S11
production-schedule encode section was **0.664 ms p95**.

## Research and measurement choices

- Godot's [`Time.get_ticks_usec`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/classes/class_time.html#class-time-method-get-ticks-usec)
  is monotonic and is the engine API used around each subsystem. Its integer microsecond
  granularity is sufficient here. An empty adjacent-read bracket measured
  **0.000/0.001/0.001/0.246 ms median/p95/p99/worst** over 30,000 pooled samples.
- Godot's [CPU optimization guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/performance/cpu_optimization.html)
  recommends profiling before optimization and distinguishes script, physics and rendering
  costs. This fixture records explicit script-owner sections; it is not a replacement for
  production profiler captures.
- Godot's [idle and physics processing documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/scripting/idle_and_physics_processing.html)
  defines fixed-rate physics callbacks. The prototype advances deterministic 60 Hz rules in
  the same order each tick but accelerates wall time, so it measures work rather than pacing.
- Glenn Fiedler's [Fix Your Timestep](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/)
  explains why fixed simulation steps need enough headroom to avoid falling behind. A median
  pass cannot compensate for sustained p95 over-subscription.
- Glenn Fiedler's [Snapshot Interpolation](https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_interpolation/)
  motivates rate selection and buffering rather than sending every complete state every
  simulation tick. S11's measured schedule remains the relevant bandwidth design.

## Options considered

| Option | Fit and trade-off | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add isolated S09/S10/S11/S12 percentile summaries | Cheap, but percentiles are not additive and do not capture allocator/cache contention or coordinator overhead. | Rejected as an integrated verdict. |
| One accelerated saved host scene with timer brackets | Runs deterministic full-cap owners sequentially for three warmup-plus-ten-minute seeds, retains every tick sample and attributes cost. It omits real pacing/network service and Jolt stepping. | **Selected for this foundation spike.** |
| Real-time production-like listen server with three clients and profiler capture | Highest fidelity for physics, transport, prediction and scheduler interaction. It needs production owners. | Required later for M1-D3, not invented inside S17. |

Production should keep one host coordinator with explicit command-before-simulation and
capture-after-simulation phases, instrument owner sections in development builds, and repeat
this budget on real production bodies/networking. Do not treat a sum of isolated subsystem
p95 values as a capacity result.

## Prototype and timing protocol

[`host.tscn`](../../tests/fixtures/s17/host.tscn) is the single saved composition. It
instances the unchanged S09 lane topology, S10/S06 pedestrian benchmark, S12 targets and
12 S05/S04 car bodies, and saves the S03 session, S12 combat owner and S05 damage owner.
The editor was unavailable. The minimal format-3 scene was text-authored under the brief's
fallback, imported with the pinned engine, and assigned retained scene/script UIDs. No
S03-S12 fixture was edited.

Each seed first advances every owner for **3,600 unmeasured ticks (60 seconds)**. Traffic,
pedestrian route/event state, combat cadence/history and codec paths are warmed. S10 owns a
continuous local tick domain: warmup is `-3600..-1` and measurement is `0..35999`. Traffic,
combat and synthetic rows retain their `0..39599` absolute timeline. This lets S10 evaluate
car-contact death/replenishment through measured tick 35,699 and apply only its intended
300-tick terminal suppression. Runtime assertions require a `-1` to `0` boundary, continuous
owner ticks and a final measured tick of 35,999; all seed receipts passed.

The fixture clears timing and owner receipt counters only, retaining warmed simulation state.
S05 starts a fresh trial session at the boundary because each independent chain trial
necessarily restores its 12 live cars. Measurement covers **36,000 ticks (10 minutes)**.

[`S17Host`](../../tests/fixtures/s17/host.gd) measures this order:

1. S10 normal-play event, graph route, local separation, graph-kinematic movement and
   death/replenishment work for 64 slots;
2. S09 reservation, following, blockage recovery and unchanged `S04DriveRules.advance`
   for 24 moving cars (eight parked cars do no controller work);
3. S12 target history plus one scripted validated/rewound shot per player every 15 ticks;
4. S05 chain work, with one 12-car chain restarted every 5,000 measured ticks (**eight per
   seed, 24 measured trials total**); and
5. one complete 148-row S11 encode repeated independently for each of three clients.

The conservative `total` bracket starts before pedestrians and ends immediately after
snapshot encoding. Elapsed values are held in scalar locals. Growing-array appends, chain
receipt retention and timing-summary work occur only after the bracket closes. Timer calls
and scalar assignments remain as unavoidable instrumentation and are quantified by the
empty-bracket baseline above.

After the conservative bracket closes, the fixture measures S11's selected schedule: one
74-row subset encoded once for broadcast every four ticks, alternating subsets so each row
is revisited every eight ticks. `total_production_schedule` is the same tick's conservative
total minus conservative snapshot duration plus selected-schedule snapshot duration. Row
construction is included in both snapshot durations. Complete raw samples for both lines are
retained.

Fixed population rows include four players, 16 synthetic moving rockets, 16 retained wrecks
and 16 retained dead pedestrians. Rockets/retained rows exercise codec cardinality only;
they have no projectile, collision or presentation simulation.

### S17-local adapter seams

S09 exposes only monolithic `run()` and S10 only monolithic `run_case()`. S12's direct host
validator is behind its RPC boundary and named `_validate_intent`. Per the approved S17
integration direction, read-only adapters call the existing S09 `_reset_case`, `_spawn` and
`_step`, S10 `_reset`, `_spawn_initial_population` and `_tick`, and S12
`_validate_intent`, `_rewound_position` and `_ray_hits`. They do not copy those algorithms.
Only S10's graph-input assembly was copied line-for-line from
`tests/fixtures/s10/benchmark.gd` at base `4abc07d`, with attribution in source.

Each run first performs a 120-tick equivalence probe. S09's adapter matched public `run()`
with zero pose error and 2,880 drive-rule calls. Two separately instanced S10 saved fixtures
had zero pose error and equal metrics. S12 returned `OK`, `STALE_SEQUENCE` and `INVALID`
for valid, duplicate and nonfinite cases. These checks support fixture equivalence; private
methods are **not accepted production APIs**. Accelerated S12 work explicitly advances its
wall-clock cadence/reload gates according to simulated cadence rather than waiting in the
measured loop.

The runner stages required fixtures/assets into an addon-free project, strips main-scene,
autoload and editor-plugin settings, imports once, and starts one owned headless process per
seed. Every Godot invocation is wrapped in GNU `timeout`, with an additional Python child
wait/cleanup bound. Process count and CPU load are sampled before launch, 0.1 seconds into
startup/warmup, and after exit—never during the measured interval. Complete samples remain
in three readable per-seed JSON files; pooled nearest-rank aggregation is independently
unit-tested.

Canonical reproduction command (fresh external destination required):

```sh
export PATH="$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which godot)"):$(dirname "$(mise -C C:/GameDev/git/fun-things which gdstyle)"):$PATH"
timeout 5000 python tools/s17/run.py --godot "$(command -v godot)" \
  --output C:/tmp/ft/lanes/s17-host-tick/reproduction
```

## Results

All three processes exited zero with no engine/script diagnostics. Every equivalence and
outcome check passed. Each repetition ended with 64 pedestrian slots/live agents, at most 16
dead presentations, zero graph-legality violations, 864,000 measured S04 drive-rule calls,
zero S09 footprint collisions/gridlocks, and 9,600 measured S12 shots/rewind queries. Every
S10 receipt records continuous ticks from warmup `-1` through measured `0..35999`, with car
contact active through 35,699 and only the intended final 300 ticks suppressed. All **24
measured chains** completed 12 explosions and 144 target visits with target-work peak four
and queue peak five.

The conservative case performed 108,000 encodes and produced 216,000 packets per seed,
258,336,000 encoded bytes, with 1,196-byte packets. The S11 schedule performed 9,000
single-payload broadcasts per seed, 10,764,000 encoded bytes, also with 1,196-byte packets.
Network sending was not measured.

## Interpretation and budget proposal

This composition exposes over-subscription earlier than M1-D3, but it does not justify
lowering population, tick rate or the 4/8 ms whole-host target. The cheapest production
changes are architectural rather than balance changes:

1. **Replication:** build one immutable row set and encode one shared payload per S11 rate
   bucket; fan out the same packet bytes. Target **<=0.75 ms p95** for row capture plus all
   outgoing encodes at three clients.
2. **Pedestrians:** replace dictionary-heavy slot state with compact typed/structure-of-arrays
   data, retain staggered 10 Hz decisions, and use a reusable spatial grid/selective queries.
   Preserve 60 Hz authoritative movement/collision. Target **<=1.0 ms p95**.
3. **Traffic:** retain the measured directed-graph/S04 path and target **<=1.5 ms p95** at
   24 moving cars. Use indexed lane/conflict occupancy before reducing fidelity if the real
   body implementation exceeds it.
4. Reserve **<=0.25 ms p95** for four-player combat/history, **<=0.10 ms p95** for chain/
   lifecycle work, and the remaining **0.40 ms** for body physics, prediction replay,
   session work and coordinator overhead. This reserve is narrow; production profiling may
   require reducing the first three shares rather than relaxing the whole-host budget.

These shares sum to 4 ms and are ceilings inside the whole-host budget, not independently
spendable allowances. p99 must remain <=8 ms and production should track worst/hitch causes
without redefining p99 around them.

## Proven and not proven

**Proven within this fixture**

- one saved composition executes full declared population cardinality after a retained
  60-second warmup for three deterministic ten-minute measured seeds;
- S10's owner-local domain is continuous from warmup `-3600..-1` into measurement
  `0..35999`, preserving its intended 35,700 active car-contact ticks and 300 terminal ticks;
- S09/S10/S12 private-seam adapters reproduce short source-fixture outcomes before timing;
- 24 traffic controllers call unchanged S04 rules once per measured tick while eight parked
  rows do no AI;
- eight measured S05 12-car chains per seed finish through the unchanged damage/chain owner;
- four scripted players traverse S12's shape/admission/sequence/cadence/ammo validator and
  target-history query path without a network transport;
- the conservative and S11-scheduled codec paths remain <=1,200 bytes per packet; and
- under observed contention, both cases miss p95, while only the conservative case misses
  p99; pedestrian work is the largest attributed p95 section.

**Not proven**

- quiet Windows or any Linux timing, named target-hardware acceptance, real-time pacing, or a
  graphical/exported process;
- engine physics/Jolt stepping per accelerated tick, 32 moving `CharacterBody3D` contacts,
  selective pedestrian sweeps, player movement/prediction/replay or actual rocket motion;
- socket/ENet service, packet send, transport allocations, bandwidth or adverse delivery;
- production row reuse, real state schema or lifecycle journal;
- four-client joins, reset, seats, damage handoff, AI/player contacts, rendering, animation,
  audio or VFX; or
- a production public per-tick integration contract. Underscore-prefixed fixture units are
  explicitly bounded measurement adapters, not architecture to ship.

The fixture overestimates production snapshot CPU in its conservative line and underestimates
total host work because it omits real body physics, prediction, sockets and projectiles.
Those effects cannot be assumed to cancel.

## M1 production requirements

1. Add public per-tick APIs for traffic, pedestrians and combat validation/history; preserve
   one owner per field and remove production dependency on S17's private fixture seams.
2. Define and enforce one host phase order: accepted commands, AI decisions, shared movement/
   physics, damage/lifecycle, then state capture/replication.
3. Retain per-owner and inclusive tick instrumentation in development builds with bounded
   sample export and nearest-rank median/p95/p99/worst aggregation.
4. Implement shared immutable S11 row capture/packet reuse plus measured rate buckets; include
   reliable lifecycle separately and never trade correctness for encode budget silently.
5. Implement compact pedestrian state/indexed occupancy and traffic lane/conflict indexing,
   then measure actual selective queries and CharacterBody contacts at full caps.
6. Integrate prediction replay, four player streams, damage, rockets, chains, seats, retained
   entities and reset without duplicate side effects.
7. Run at least three real-time ten-minute measured seeds after 60-second warmups on named
   quiet Windows and Linux M1 hardware, then repeat normal/adverse three-client ENet profiles.
8. Keep whole-host p95 <=4 ms and p99 <=8 ms. Optimize ownership/data/query work first;
   changing caps, tick rate or budgets requires owner review.
