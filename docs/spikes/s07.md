Primary sources checked **7 October 2026**. Local installed executable reports
`4.8.dev7.official.c971f93e7`; [dev7 release](https://godotengine.org/article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-8-dev-7/)
identifies that snapshot. Exact inspected source ref:
`c971f93e7e76b0ef919bf6009e7b868bea04db7f` (**G7** below).
The [official archive](https://godotengine.org/download/archive/) currently lists
**4.7.2 stable (18 August 2026)** and **4.8-dev7 development (29 September 2026)**.
`latest` documentation is unstable and may move; exact G7 sources settle pin claims.
The [compact source receipt](s07-evidence/research.json) records refs, URLs,
inspected symbols and SHA-256s without copying engine manuals into the repository.

| Mechanism | Supported meaning and limits for this investigation |
| --- | --- |
| Mip-level texture streaming | Introduced in [4.8-dev5](https://godotengine.org/article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-8-dev-5/), present in G7. Experimental `TextureStreaming`/`StreamedTexture2D` use shader feedback to change loaded mip levels. Opt in to `rendering/textures/streaming/enabled`, restart editor and use the **Texture2D Streamed** importer. G7 defaults streaming and its memory-budget enforcement to false; the project's settings contain no opt-in. This is a supported candidate, not an enabled/project-tested benefit. |
| Renderer scope | G7 streaming module and resource explicitly exclude `gl_compatibility`; Forward+ and Mobile shaders contain feedback paths. This source support does not prove every material/shader, graphics API, driver or export route. Verify actual streamed resources, quality convergence and costs in each selected renderer/build before relying on it. |
| Stable versus newer | The 4.7.2-stable source tree has none of the new streaming module/classes/importer. Ordinary mipmaps/compression there are not this dynamic mip residency system. Future snapshots, proposals and fixes after G7 are untested candidates and require exact refs; release/issue closure cannot update our installed binary. No upgrade follows from this research. |
| Ordinary mipmaps/compression | Mipmaps provide filtered lower-resolution samples and normally add stored levels; enabling them alone does not unload higher levels. GPU texture compression can reduce resident texture bytes/bandwidth; disk/source compression or a smaller PNG does not imply equivalent GPU savings. Import resolution, format, alpha and mip choices must be measured with visual quality. [Image import guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_images.html). |
| Resource/background loading | `ResourceLoader.load` blocks; threaded request/status/get supports loading resources in the background. Getting before completion blocks. A loaded `PackedScene` is a resource; instantiating and entering the tree are additional work, including node/component setup. [Background guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/io/background_loading.html), G7 `ResourceLoader` and `PackedScene` class contracts. Background I/O does not guarantee hitch-free instantiation, GPU upload or first use. |
| Saved scene/object residency | Application code can load/instantiate saved sectors and free their node trees. `remove_child` alone does not delete them; `queue_free` deletes at the frame boundary after deferred calls. Resources are reference counted; cached paths reuse resources while referenced. Retained scenes, definitions, preloads, callbacks or other references can keep dependencies alive after nodes go. These are building blocks for application-controlled streaming; the reviewed APIs do not select spatial sectors, preserve gameplay state or synchronize authority for us. Avoid the unqualified claim “Godot cannot stream objects.” |
| Rendering work | Automatic frustum culling, mesh LOD, visibility ranges/HLOD and optional occlusion affect drawing. They do not imply freed resources or suspended physics/AI. Occlusion adds CPU work and needs useful camera-dependent coverage; vertical city views/tall roofs require proof. Shared mesh/material reuse may allow renderer batching; exact batching varies. MultiMesh supplies GPU instancing but indexes a group as one object, trading individual visibility/lighting/identity control for lower submission overhead. None is selected here. |

## Bounded experiment method

Commission the smallest saved two-sector S06 intersection with linked committed
Blender/GLB fixtures, accepted S02 camera/actual controller, S04 car/body envelope
and representative collision/topology. Reuse accepted imports; add only missing
representative height/material/texture/animation variety through asset handoffs.
Later compose saved baseline layouts through the editor: two-sector calibration,
then the six-block reference at declared density. Buildings/roads/props are authored
prefab instances with saved transforms; no benchmark-generated render meshes or
runtime city authoring. Dynamic actors may be instantiated from saved prefabs
through their simulation owners. Moving dummy stand-ins are a rendering diagnostic,
never evidence for AI/physics/network capacity.

First experiment: baseline representativeness, repeatability and limiting cost.
Second: **one** growth axis selected from the baseline's risk, with a modest step
(e.g. 1×, 2×, then 4× only while within stop limits). Do not cross every axis into a
large benchmark matrix. Stop within the assigned 1–2 days; record inconclusive or
unsupported dimensions and propose a separately budgeted next experiment.

| Axis | Controlled change and required accounting |
| --- | --- |
| Layout extent | First hold content counts/variety/population constant and vary saved spacing/bounds to isolate coordinates, traversal and visibility. Separately grow authored sectors at fixed density: extent and total object/resident count necessarily grow together; report both. Growth copies have distinct placed IDs/connectivity, not duplicated spawn identity. |
| Density/object work | At fixed bounds/asset variety, increase saved prefab count; count nodes, surfaces, colliders and active bodies separately. Declare distribution/visible count; a million empty metres is not city capacity. |
| Asset variety/residency | At fixed placement/count, increase unique models/materials/textures with comparable draw complexity; record shared versus unique bytes, formats/resolution/mips and reference roots. Repeated tiny palettes underestimate production residency. |
| Simulation | Keep static content fixed; vary active actor/car/controller/query/navigation work and effects within the provisional global caps first. Any diagnostic above those caps is a separate planning load, never a runtime cap change or M1 promise. |
| Views/lifecycle | Same content with players together then four separated views, one local rig per process; fixed route/speed, seed and event schedule. Four views are four processes, not split screen. Measure graphical host separately from each client and standalone. |

Run cards name engine/templates/build hash, content manifest/scene revision and
asset fingerprints, OS/driver/CPU/GPU/RAM/storage, renderer/API/quality/resolution,
power/thermal state, process roles, seed, density/unique variety, sector/node/body/
nav counts, camera projection/height/frustum, player distribution, speed and route.
Record which subsystem is real, proxy, missing or disabled. Fully loaded sectors
are the baseline. Use S05's real effect/12-car burst when measuring that dimension;
keep 64 pedestrians, 32 live cars plus four players and the design's retained/
projectile/cosmetic caps as the initial target, globally rather than per view.
A phase-zero fixture cannot establish integrated M1-D3 acceptance.

Use 60 s warmup, at least 10 min measured normal/capacity route and 20 burst trials
per run, following the design envelope. Plan three repeat runs per selected case;
retain per-run samples/variance rather than only pooled averages. Compare uncapped
headroom and 60-capped presentation with the same quality/content. Separate cold
first use from warm cache runs, recording restart/cache conditions; do not silently
flush system caches or count warmed editor data as a cold exported launch.
Use exported graphical builds when S08's exact templates/recipe exist; editor runs
are labeled diagnostics. Import/export authoring costs are recorded separately.
Desktop ENet processes can prepare four-view cases once real actors exist; real
Steam route and physical Deck cases remain deferred, with no simulated substitute.

Routes include fast S04 driving, foot traversal, reverse-direction seams, towers
below/near/above camera height, abrupt view changes, sector crossings and clustered
transition/effect bursts. Repeat chains off-camera and confirm authoritative outcomes.
Join during/after bursts, reset, leave/rejoin and saved scene unload/reload diagnostics
compare the same settled point after ten cycles. Initial fully loaded M1 does not
unload sectors during travel; an isolated resource-lifetime diagnostic may free and
reload a saved fixture after safe teardown, without inventing a streaming protocol.

## Measurements, tools and stopping rules

Retain full-interval p50/p95/p99/max and raw frame/physics samples, including bursts;
startup/loading gets separate timelines. Use Godot profiler/visual profiler/monitors
for attribution and engine render/node/resource counts, an OS frame-pacing capture
and per-process memory sampling for exported runs, and GPU-tool captures where
supported. Name tools/versions, intervals and overhead; repeat a representative
capture without heavy profiling to estimate distortion. Headless results can
isolate server work but cannot establish graphical host or GPU capacity.
See [Profiler](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/debug/the_profiler.html)
and [Debugger panels](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/debug/debugger_panel.html).
Unavailable GPU telemetry is a named gap, never zero cost.

| Record | Minimum observations / owner threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Frame/physics | CPU main/render-thread and GPU frame time, physics plus AI/query/chain time, pacing/hitch duration/frequency and dropped intervals. Design provisional frame p95 ≤16.67 ms, p99 ≤20 ms, sustained 60 FPS; host simulation p95 ≤4 ms, p99 ≤8 ms at fixed 60 Hz. CPU and GPU overlap: do not sum them as a frame estimate. |
| Rendering/content | Draw calls, surfaces/material switches, rendered geometry/triangles, shadows/lights, transparent overdraw, unique meshes/materials/textures and imported/source/export bytes. Record visible versus resident objects and per-camera bounds/culling/LOD correctness; geometry/draw counts alone cannot pass frame budgets. |
| Residency/lifetime | OS process resident/peak RAM, engine resource/object/node counts and texture/mesh/video allocation estimates; streamer counter only if actually enabled. Provisional ≤2 GiB per-process resident and ≤1 GiB observed GPU allocation; on Deck these overlap shared RAM, so report accounting domains/tool limitations rather than adding them. Track live references/cache hits, pending loads, retained resources and settled counts after ten cycles; explain persistent caches/driver allocations and reject sustained unexplained growth. |
| Loading/traversal | Cold/warm startup, resource load versus instantiate/tree-entry/GPU-first-use, first-visit hitch, seam/reload bursts, join baseline/apply and teardown; import/reimport/bake/export duration and artifact size separately. API draft world-ready ≤30 s, baseline ≤1 MiB, apply/transfer ≤10 s and handoff ≤5 s. No invented import/export deadline: report cost and propose an owner decision if material. |
| Authority/network | Active bodies/collision pairs, navigation/path requests and bake size, AI/query rates/time, populations/retained states, serialization/tick/queue peaks, baseline/journal bytes, per-client and aggregate host wire bandwidth including transport overhead. Use design bandwidth worst 10 s windows and API queue/deadline limits; verify off-camera outcomes, distinct spawns/current join state and prediction/collision fences where implemented. Missing production AI/codec/lifecycle cases remain M1 follow-ups. |

For a target row, pass requires repeated correct outcomes and all applicable owner
budgets, with margin reported as budget minus measured value (and measurement
variance). A desktop row is only a desktop observation. At a repeated budget breach,
sustained pacing failure, memory pressure, unstable lifetimes, content/authority error
or invalid telemetry, stop increasing that axis; retain the last passing and first
failing row. Stop immediately on correctness/identity/collision failures. A single
noisy run gets one controlled rerun, not indefinite tuning. Diagnose one bottleneck
and test one cheap change against the same baseline; escalate budget/scope/config
choices to the named owner. If all tested points pass, report **at least the tested
envelope**, never an extrapolated maximum. Bound total saved experiment content and
run counts in the launch card; no allocation-to-crash search.

## Investigation and organization decisions

Start with measured attribution, then test the cheapest relevant intervention.
Texture bytes: reuse materials/textures, appropriate import resolution/compression/
mips and remove accidental unique references; only then test opt-in G7 mip streaming
against quality/pop-in/I/O/CPU/GPU cost and budget behavior. Render submission:
reuse mesh/materials, remove excessive surfaces, check frustum/AABBs/shadows,
then camera-appropriate LOD/ranges or occluders. Occlusion must save more than its
CPU cost; towers cannot hide actors/roads. Compare Mobile/Forward+ on named desktop
as preparation; Deck renderer selection stays S07/S08 device evidence.

Simulation/query/navigation: inspect active work and S06 collision/route boundaries,
static shape complexity, path-request frequency and owner caps before scheduling
changes. Network: profile serialization, payload/refresh/baseline size before new
relevance policies; preserve reliable lifecycle and S03's per-entity refresh rule.
Loading/node churn: fix unnecessary resource duplication/references and excessive
saved composition granularity before considering background loading or pooling.
Pooling is warranted only by demonstrated repeated allocation/instantiation cost
and safe reset/identity contracts; it retains memory and can worsen residency.
Batching/MultiMesh needs demonstrated draw/submission pressure, spatial group bounds
and preserved Blender source/placement/identity contracts; no generated mesh shortcut.
Actual scene/resource unloading becomes a separate bounded decision only when
resident bytes or load/first-use cost remains the limit after cheap measures, with
proof that reload meets traversal lead time and required gameplay stays coherent.
No cache/manager framework, procedural layout or heavy streaming system is preselected.
A measured **no change** decision is a valid result.

Support future growth through existing [scene](../scene-structure.md),
[asset](../assets.md) and [ownership](../architecture.md) contracts. Reusable imported
prefabs own visuals/components; saved sectors own placement/anchors; CityData owns
connectivity and derived revisions, and Match owns dynamic outcomes. One integrator
owns each sector; avoid one giant inseparable import and equally avoid arbitrary
per-object sectors. S06 chooses seam/topology ownership; S07 measures candidate
render/resource granularity without redesigning topology. Document truly shared
immutable models/materials/definitions versus sector-local references, reverse
dependencies and load/release roots. Fully loaded district scene references may keep
sector resources resident: organization alone is not unloading. Preserve stable
world/sector/link IDs, import UIDs, inherited metadata and bake fingerprints; derived
nav/occlusion/map data stays valid for its geometry/topology lifetime.

Treat render visibility, simulation scheduling, network relevance and resource
residency as four distinct decisions. Clients submit intent; authority continues
collision, AI, damage, population and world state off-camera. Local culling cannot
remove required bodies or routes, replenish duplicate spawns, erase wrecks, or
change global caps. Any future unload/relevance proposal must name durable-state
ownership, dependency/collision readiness, stale generation/revision/callback
rejection, and late-join/reload/prediction fences under the existing
[API](../api-contracts.md#admission-and-replication) and
[multiplayer](../multiplayer.md) contracts. Specify only the risk and required proof
here; do not design a streaming replication protocol.

Coordinate precision is another axis. Record saved bounds/max distance from origin,
camera near/far planes, physics/query and navigation region bounds. Compare the same
fixture near the origin and at a selected farther offset only if planned extent or
visible jitter/contact/path/camera symptoms warrant it. The
[large-world guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/physics/large_world_coordinates.html)
explains single-precision vector limits, double-precision build/template/extension
costs and multiplayer complications of origin shifting. Its illustrative ranges
are not our measured map-capacity limit. The current reference district gives no
evidence to change precision builds or origins. Record the exact pin/backend and
symptom before a separately reviewed precision/origin investigation.
