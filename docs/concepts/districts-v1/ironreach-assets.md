# Ironreach asset breakdown

The owner likes [revision 02](08-ironreach-v02.png), including its selected
chain-link enclosures and more rundown character, and requests the asset registry
update. The [central registry](asset-register.md#08-ironreach-assets) records each
required design once and identifies its district uses. This breakdown explains
the set; quantities, dimensions and individual asset designs remain to refine.

not 36 unique buildings or a separate landmark model. The main yard combines
unequal members from the same family. Refine common wall bays, roller-door
surrounds, roof sections and office interfaces; compatible dimensions and joins
still require a closer concept. Normal windows, shutters, gutters and trim belong
to the building brief rather than duplicate standalone prop records.

## Wear and material variations

Use faded petrol paint, mismatched corrugated roof patches, localized rust,
weathered brick bases, scuffed furniture and faded signage. These are required
finish treatments owned by the relevant building, prop and graphic briefs.
Reuse finish variations on the same parts rather than making clean and worn
copies of every model. Keep broad forms and warm functioning doorways readable.
The shared public boardwalk retains its citywide treatment.

## Supporting items and exclusions

Utility cabinets, drain covers, separate closed service doors, public bins,
bollards, low service barriers, wheel stops, low parking signs and a straight
bench are recorded as supporting uses to confirm in a closer layout. Standalone
service doors must not duplicate doors already included in the building designs.
Formal planters are not required for this district.

Work-apron graphics remain candidates pending the closer yard layout and the
boundary with future surface tooling. Road markings, parking asphalt and road
surfaces are not required by this pass. Yard concrete is a visual material brief,
not a new road mesh kit. No modelling or implementation is included.

Shared shoreline corner/end connections also use `city_shore_edges.04`.
Open-sea appearance uses the shared `city_water_look.01` visual study.
The central registry records these uses without new district-specific geometry.
