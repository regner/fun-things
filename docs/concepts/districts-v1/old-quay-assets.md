# Old Quay building and prop breakdown

This is the detailed asset breakdown of [Old Quay v03](05-old-quay-v03.png).
The [central tracker](asset-register.md) remains the single live source for district
demand and progress. Every design below has a linked brief. The harbour direction
is positively received; docks/yachts and the shared pedestrian connection are
requested. The detailed set is recorded as required demand; individual designs remain to
refine before any later production.

Repeated buildings, lamps and boats reuse designs. Six illustrated yachts need two
proposed yacht designs, not six unique models. Road surfaces remain excluded.

v03 does not clearly establish it as another required design. The civic square is
an arrangement of shared paving, seating and open space, not a separate building.
The exact hall site needs fitting to the map: the rendering drifted west of the
proposed northern placeholder 07. Building counts are not taken from the raster.

## Tracking and scope

retain one central entry with the required Old Quay uses now recorded, alongside
the requested dock/yacht kit and pedestrian bridge. Supporting uses remain to confirm.
Building and prop design selection, exact sizes and placed quantities remain open.
No modelling, scene editing or implementation was performed for this breakdown.
