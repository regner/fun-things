# Northpoint asset breakdown

Based on [revision 04](01-northpoint-v04.png), including the restored university
hall, retained sports pavilion and requested lighthouse. The owner requested a
district asset pass; the [central registry](asset-register.md#01-northpoint-assets)
records the resulting needed/candidate uses. This page explains the design set.
Individual asset designs, exact dimensions and placed counts still need refinement.

## Buildings: five reusable designs

| Design | Brief and included parts |
| --- | --- |
| Main university hall | [Academic buildings](../../assets/d01_academic_buildings.md), `.01`; brick/stone hall, connected U-shaped wings, entrance arch, doors/windows, pitched roofs, clock tower and static clock-face appearance |
| Long teaching wing | Same brief, `.02`; standalone elongated institutional building, with restrained roof/entrance variants |
| Compact academic annex | Same brief, `.03`; smaller supporting buildings around courts; roof/footprint variants share the family |
| Curved arena-like sports pavilion | [Sports pavilion](../../assets/d01_sports_pavilion.md), `.01`; preserve the liked curved roof and field-side position, with facade, entrances and normal trim included |
| Coastal lighthouse | [Lighthouse](../../assets/d01_lighthouse.md), `.01`; one assembly with tower, base entry, lantern, gallery railing and cap |

The university wings and clock tower belong to its main-hall assembly, not extra
standalone buildings. The sports pavilion is the arena-looking building already
present, not a request for another arena. A separate pavilion end-wing remains
optional. The lighthouse does not require a keeper's cottage or functional beacon.

## Sports ground and campus finishes

| Required asset | Brief |
| --- | --- |
| Oval track appearance and lane markings | [Sports artwork](../../assets/d01_sports_surface.md), `.01`; warm track finish and lane pattern |
| Field line artwork | Same brief, `.02`; markings on the shared grass appearance |
| Quiet paving | [Ground finishes](../../assets/city_ground_finishes.md), `.01`; quadrangle, paths, entry aprons and lighthouse approach |
| Grass and planted-soil finishes | Same brief, `.04–.05`; courts, field and garden beds |

The track and open courts are layouts using these surfaces, not separate duplicate
ground models. No regulation track size is accepted from the concept. Smaller
court graphics remain a candidate: the university revision displaced the earlier
small court. Football goals, nets, hoops, grandstands and stadium floodlights are
not established by the current overview and are not added as required assets.
Road surfaces remain excluded for future automatic tooling.

## Shared structures and props

| Required asset | Brief and reuse |
| --- | --- |
| Complete coastal boardwalk kit | [Boardwalk](../../assets/city_boardwalk.md), `.01–.05`; same straight/bend deck, fascia, support and shore-transition designs as The Crescents |
| Low seaward rail | [Quay furniture](../../assets/city_quay_furniture.md), `.02`; shared boardwalk railing; lighthouse gallery rail stays in the tower assembly |
| Low seawall and rock shore | [Shore edges](../../assets/city_shore_edges.md), `.01`, `.03`; underneath/beside the walking deck |
| Street, pedestrian and entrance lights | [City lights](../../assets/city_lights.md), `.01`, `.02`, `.04`; reduce the image's overly dense spacing during refinement |
| Low shrubs and broad-canopy tree | [Planting](../../assets/city_planting.md), `.03–.04`; shared kit for courts, entries and shore access |
| Straight bench | [Seating](../../assets/city_seating.md), `.01`; quiet court/boardwalk seating with a clear walking line |
| Campus entry graphics and wall panel | [Campus graphics](../../assets/d01_campus_graphics.md), `.02`, on [shared panel](../../assets/city_sign_supports.md), `.01`; institution names/copy remain provisional |

The central registry owns current boardwalk users. It now covers Northpoint,
The Crescents, Terrace Ward, Glassward, Broadlot and Ironreach. East Docks is
excluded, with terminals in neighbouring districts; Old Quay route fit remains
open. The overview does not validate the remaining transitions.
Ordinary roof planes, dormers and institutional facade details belong
to the building designs unless later asset sheets justify a separate reusable part.

Other unselected shared candidates retain their existing tracker entries; they are
not automatically commissioned by this pass. Cars and people reuse city actor and
vehicle tracks. All required designs have briefs. No modelling, scene editing,
road tooling or implementation occurred.

Shared shoreline corner/end connections also use `city_shore_edges.04`.
Open-sea appearance uses the shared `city_water_look.01` visual study.
The central registry records these uses without new district-specific geometry.
