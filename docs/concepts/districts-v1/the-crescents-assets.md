# The Crescents and boardwalk asset breakdown

The owner likes [revision 02](02-the-crescents-v02.png) and requests the asset set.
The [central tracker](asset-register.md) records required district uses and remaining
candidates. This page explains the set; it does not duplicate live status tracking.
Counts below describe reusable designs, not the number of buildings in the image.

## Buildings and fittings

| Required design | Coverage and brief |
| --- | --- |

These four building designs are the core set. A bespoke compact corner home and
shop rear annex remain optional candidates. Garden plots, the cul-de-sac and its
turning head are arrangements, not additional building assets. The turning head's
planted centre uses shared planting; road surfaces stay outside this work.

to identify reliably in this overview. Notice artwork reuses shared sign hardware.
No gate mechanics, interiors, play equipment or bespoke cul-de-sac sculpture is
implied. Cars and people remain existing shared actor/vehicle tracks.

The five boardwalk members form one shared kit. The central registry records
current coastal users and the exclusion of East Docks. The selected image shows
the western/southwestern stretch continuing beyond the crop; it does not validate
all remaining transitions. The route ends on either side of East Docks rather
than completing a timber ring through the working freight shore.

Keep the timber treatment consistent, preserve seawall/rock and avoid filling the
sea. Boardwalk supports do not own the shoreline. Marina pontoons remain a separate
functional-looking kit for boats; no boardwalk ring around the inner basin is implied.

## Fit and next work

The new eastern cul-de-sac enters from the southern curve, terminates inside the
housing pocket and has a pedestrian continuation. Its turning head and adjusted
house plots are concept changes only. The exact map extract still shows the saved
layout without that addition. Counts, carriageway clearances, deck width and support
spacing require later fit work; they cannot be measured reliably from this raster.

Asset concepts can now refine the four building types, domestic details and shared
boardwalk kit. All listed components have briefs; no modelling, saved-scene editing,
road tooling or implementation was performed.

Shared shoreline corner/end connections also use `city_shore_edges.04`.
Open-sea appearance uses the shared `city_water_look.01` visual study.
The central registry records these uses without new district-specific geometry.
