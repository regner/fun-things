## Self-review

The three sheets were visually inspected. Clothing, empty/unarmed hands, civilian
bags, head shapes and broad silhouettes fit ordinary city residents. Rounded forms
and subdued petrol areas relate to the accepted city; accent colours carry the
clothing masses without emissive character materials. B is the strongest contrast
to A/C through hair, coat length and bag placement. A/C still overlap as short-jacket
men; hair/cap and garment proportions must preserve their distinction if both are
ever commissioned. Player/civilian colour distinction awaits the selected player.

All sheets overdescribe curls, folds, seams and footwear. Production should reduce
these to broad sculpted forms and a small opaque palette, rather than copying fine
raster detail. B's hair could hide shoulder colour from directly above; its tote
needs separation from the leg through walk/run/death. A's bag should remain close
to the hip and avoid arm intersections. C's cap and broad shoulders need a clear
neck/head boundary in overhead poses.

The larger overhead panels are useful enlarged shape studies. The tiny direction
strips do **not** reliably depict their labelled compass rotations, and B includes
oblique facial visibility. No strip establishes a north-up camera, animation or
measured pixel-size result. The sheets are generated raster references, not actual
Godot captures, dimension measurements, model turnarounds or rig/clip evidence.
The approved asset must be checked at vertical-down perspective, 47 m height,
42° FOV, 1280×800, north-up, including rest and idle/walk/run/death poses.
