A — Compact combustion Recommended

Ivory/amber flash, tiny sparks, short warm trail, separated coral/orange lobes. Closest fit to the established fire language and strongest contrast against neon signs.

B — Neon ion

Cyan-white forks, coral/violet petals and broken impact arcs. Strong sci-fi identity; could compete with cyan and magenta streets and suggest energized weapons.

C — Arcade pop

Butter-yellow pops, uneven orange droplets and chunky scalloped lobes. Bouncier irreverent tone; petals need tighter shaping to avoid a flower silhouette.
