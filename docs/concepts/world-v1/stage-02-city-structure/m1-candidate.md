# Selected M1 location — Signal Row / Ironreach edge

9 October 2026 · **Selected by the owner; six-block programme retained.**

[Selected city structure](01-neighbourhood-loops.png) · [Full Stage 2 brief](README.md)

The owner selected the yellow six-block area on map A, spanning the commercial/workshop
transition and touching the downtown and retail edges. This is one compact exterior
playable district, not a commitment to build four full named districts. All city
and district names remain placeholders.

The area can represent the chosen city direction through commercial signage, a
nearby tower, ordinary housing, workshops, parked cars and contrasting open space.
The campus, harbour bridge and shipping docks remain parts of the whole-city vision;
this recommendation does not require them inside the first playable area.

## Fit to the existing six-block brief

Carry forward the original content programme. The rows are themes to accommodate,
not a new detailed block layout, asset list or approved production placement.

| Existing block theme | Fit within the proposed area |
| --- | --- |
| Shops and office-tower edge | Signal Row frontage near the Glassward transition, with a foot passage |
| Workshops | Low roofs and working courts toward Ironreach |
| Central plaza | A compact public space within the commercial area, with multiple walking exits |
| Depot and service alley | A connected service escape between commercial and workshop streets |
| Housing and apartment-tower edge | A mixed-use residential pocket, rather than importing a full housing district |
| Repair / chain yard | Open service or retail-edge parking with two exits and space for car clusters |

Keep two connected driving loops, pedestrian alternatives and the established
ordinary 9 m carriageway / 4 m sidewalks as the starting geometry. The first layout
must show where those routes fit; a macro route passing through the yellow rectangle
does not by itself provide two playable loops.

## Extent and limits

The current yellow area is a **260 × 163 m size study**, with six 64 × 56 m reference
lots; the earlier surrounding buffer would make 284 × 187 m. Its positions are
approximate. Selecting this area should retain a roughly six-block scope while
allowing lot edges and local routes to follow the selected city structure, rather
than forcing the old rectangular grid over it. Material extent changes must return
for review.

The site avoids making the harbour bridge a prerequisite for first playable movement.
Its main risk is fitting residential variety, useful open space and tower exposure
into a small mixed-use area. Larger streets shown in the city diagram are roles and
centre lines, not a promise of extra lanes.

The area is inland, so island-wide water containment does not solve the M1 boundary.
Later layout work must provide returning routes and deliberate authored limits
without relying on access to the unbuilt remainder of the city. No new travel,
streaming or population system is proposed. The current population and blast rules
remain unchanged.

**Owner choice recorded:** this commercial/workshop transition is the M1 location,
carrying forward the six-block programme above. Exact local layout,
district boundary refinement and measured gameplay validation remain subsequent work.
