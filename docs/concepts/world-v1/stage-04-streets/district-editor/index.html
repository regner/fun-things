Redraw the districts

Quick instructions

Select a district, then drag its round points. Double-click an edge to insert a point on it.

Add point: click anywhere to insert a corner after the nearest edge. Select a point and press Delete to remove it.

Scroll to zoom. Use Pan map, or hold Space and drag to move the map. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes.

District polygons are independent: shared edges are not linked, so edits can leave overlaps or gaps. Fills are masked to land; exported points keep your full polygon.

Coordinates are concept-map metres: X right/east, Y down/south, origin at the map’s upper left. These are planning polygons, not saved Godot geometry.
